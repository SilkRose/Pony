# (Un)Happy New Yuri Contest Silk Rose Blurbs

### [A Moment in Eternity](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571506/a-moment-in-eternity)
I agree with the author here, writing is hard. But they did an amazing job here, they painted a picture that brought tears to my eyes. A picture as beautiful as Rarity, and as ever lasting as Twilight. They captured the emotions and weight of lose amazingly and without dawdling. The pairing is done well and stays true to both mares' personalities. I loved this story and will be taking inspiration from it in future. Thank you [Photon76](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/340163/Photon76) for writing this wonderful story.

### [Not her. Never her.](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571738/not-her-never-her)
This story shows what can happen when a pony refuses to give up on their love, consequences be damned. The author did an amazing job showing the visceral emotions of love, and the downfall of not letting go. The ever present mantra of the title, "Not her, Never her." acts as the beating heart of the story and the monster it's shown through. The pairing is done great despite one of them being an un-dead monster. Thank you [DapperLilArts](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/696472/DapperLilArts) for writing this visceral story.

### [Love Thyself (Cinderblox)](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/570865/love-thyself-cinderblox)
Slow and methodical, this story brings you on a trot down the beach, much like in the story. The out of order chapters telling a story unto themselves, coupled with the duality of the real world versus the dream world, makes this story very unique. The emotional turmoil in Luna was handled with grace and care. The pairing is unique and done well. The story is great, and I would gladly trot with it again down that beach. Thank you [Equimorto](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/289771/Equimorto) for writing this amazing story.

### [The Party Will Still Be There](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/569726/the-party-will-still-be-there)
Showing what could happen if the rest of the mane six also had an extended lifespan, this story pulls at my heart strings with my favorite pairing in the mane six. I'm glad that the party will still be there, but also, that Pinkie Pie will be as well. Twilight acts like a bird with Pinkie her prey. Pinkie may never love Twilight in the same way, but I will always love this story. Thank you [BifauxnenStroganoff](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/18580/BifauxnenStroganoff) for writing this emotional story.

### [Disparate Pen Pals](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571169/disparate-pen-pals)
This story takes a unique approach to one of my favorite story types, the letter story. The increasing emotion and desperation in the letters as they went on was done amazingly. The replies from the at first mystery reply-er were very cute and heartfelt. The pairing sounds cute, but sadly they had little interaction in the story. I loved the ending and hope the letters find there way to her. Thank you [Rego](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/180061/Rego) for writing this adorable story.

### [The First One's Free](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/569158/the-first-ones-free)
This story shows addiction in a very real light. Sunset taking advantage of Pinkie hurt, but is all too realistic. The desperation in Sunset and the Worry in Pinkie escalating as the story progressed was handled excellently. I loved this pairing, especially Pinkie Pie. The escape mechanic is great and very on the nose for a drug allegory. The ending shows Pinkie's vulnerability and Sunset's willingness to abuse on full display. Thank you [TCC56](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/350373/TCC56) for writing this relatable story.

### [Better Than You](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/569455/better-than-you)
Filled with angst, this story is good enough, and I'm all here for it. The tone being set from the very first line, fills the reader with said angst. The low self-esteems and their comparisons to each other bringing them down is executed well. The pairing is great and one I love. The ending was really cute, especially the kiss. I hope they go on to live a happy life together! Thank you [restless0](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/638217/restless0) for writing this angst filled story.

### [An (Un)happy Hearth's Warming](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/569320/an-unhappy-hearths-warming)
This story lives up to its name with a gut punch of an ending you know is coming the whole time reading it. The slow pacing acting as the wind up is the only preparation you get. I loved Rarity's characterization and all the presents she got her friends. Truthfully, I love all the characters in this one. The pairing is one I love, but sadly didn't work out here. Thank you [KotaBugVO](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/397468/KotaBugVO) for writing this (un)happy story.

### [I'm Not Her.](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/570610/im-not-her)
Showing what happens when you let something go on for too long, this story shows Twilight at her breaking point. The characters are done amazingly. Twilight's straightforward attitude and hard love towards Sunset is great. Sunset's slow breakdown is handled with care. The idea of Sunset loving the other Twilight is really interesting and handled great. The pairing is good, and one of my favorites. The ending is sad and done well. Thank you [PI](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/241272/PI_) for writing this sad story.

### [sunsetshimmer.exe](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/570289/sunsetshimmerexe)
This story is unique, having the POV character be a robot who is being worked on throughout the story. The changes and upgrades to her mind and body telling their own story alongside the one of Twilight and her love for Sunset. Replacing your ex-girlfriend with a robot you made and programmed is dedication… Perhaps in the wrong direction though. The characters are great, and the ship is awesome! The ending leaves me wanting more and for her to eventually be her own person. Thank you [Oroboro](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/163197/Oroboro) for writing this interesting story.

### [Fairytale of Canterlot City](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571138/fairytale-of-canterlot-city)
The difference between being responsible for someone and loving someone is a fine line that this story takes place on. Lemon's stupid actions while drunk are all too relatable, and Sunny's responsibility wearing thin isn't surprising. My first time reading this pairing, and I loved it and I plan on reading more. The characters are great. The ending was sad, but expected; it was done well too. Hopefully Lemon's arm isn't broken! Thank you [Samey90](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/112709/Samey90) for writing this festive story.

### [Leave Your Name At The Door](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571125/leave-your-name-at-the-door)
This story shows what it feels like to be lonely. Cherry finds solace in Akira for the night, and is left with only a name to remember her by. The action scene is great, while the overall pacing is slow. The pairing is unique and lovely, they play off each other perfectly. The characters are handled well. I hope the bits help Cherry out a lot! Thank you [Jarvy Jared](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/230376/Jarvy+Jared) for writing this lonely story.

### [Fidelity](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/569346/fidelity)
This story is sad, and I feel for Autumn. The descriptions of everything are so beautiful. Autumn's feelings are so raw and controlled. The custom line break is really cool. Autumn's interactions with Rainbow was nice. I love any mane six ship, but Autumn's unrequited feelings are painful and all too real. The cover is cute and pretty. The characters are all on point. The ending was sweet and done well. Thank you [pitbull-prideful](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/697467/pitbull-prideful) for writing this somber story.

### [Love In Tears](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/570596/love-in-tears)
The song in this story was beautiful, and I could practically hear Sweetie Belle singing it. Sweetie's tears feel real and I love her cat Pearl. The crystal phone is cool. The letter from Applebloom is written well and felt in character. This is the first story I've read about the CMC dating each other, sadly it didn't work out, but I hope Scootaloo and Sweetie Belle works out. Thank you [CasualBrony84](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/792928/CasualBrony84) for writing this tearful story.

### [Take Me Home](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571457/take-me-home)
The train ride while reading letters is a unique story idea that I like. I feel bad for Applejack. I'd like to think Rarity wouldn't have done this to her friends, but distance and time can do a lot of damage. Brilliant Lotus is written well and I don't like them. The pairing is one I love, but it didn't work out in this one, sadly. The ending is sad and done well. Thank you [Jhoira](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/347398/Jhoira) for writing this melancholic story.

### [Manes and Mayhem](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/570867/manes-and-mayhem)
The hero and villain romance is one I haven't read much of. I liked this one. Showing the first time the Power Ponies all met was really cool. The team being dividing on if them being together is bad or not was interesting. The Power Ponies and Mane-iac characterization is great. The pairing is unique and the obsession with manes is cute. The ending leaves me wanting more. Thank you [EpicGamer10075](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/383636/EpicGamer10075) for writing this radiant story.

### [Half](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571687/half)
Flurry Heart's infatuation with Luster is interesting. She turned to forbidden magic instead of getting over her fears and talking to her. The way Cozy easily manipulates her is done well. The spell being only half is an interesting touch. The pairing of Cozy and Luster is cute if not terrifying in this instance. Flurry trapping herself in devotion to Luster is cruel, if not warranted because she wanted it for Luster. I'd like to think Flurry's devotion to Luster would be noticed and their plot foiled. Thank you [Undome Tinwe](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/158274/Undome+Tinwe) for writing this mischievous story.

### [No Equality in Desire](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571610/no-equality-in-desire)
This story is slow and meticulous. Starlight manipulating the ponies is done excellently. While her plotting to get Sugar Belle ultimately fails due to a ghost of her past. Sunburst's appearance as a ghost haunting Starlight is great, his presence taunting her and ruining her life. The pairing is good and done well. The jealousy from Starlight and eventual manipulation of Feather Bangs is good. I'm glad it didn't work out in this case, Starlight is cruel and needed to learn her lesson. Thank you [AltruistArtist](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/338759/AltruistArtist) for writing this unequal story.

### [Let it Snow ❄️](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571558/let-it-snow)
This story is an interesting one, it brings up Christianity but doesn't say much about it other than their relationship is shunned by it. There are a few grammar and spelling mistakes throughout, which distract from the story. The contrast of the shunning religion on one side vs the abusive sisters on the other is a sad one. The pairing is unique and alright. The ending seems hopeful and is alright. I still liked this one, but wasn't the biggest fan. Thank you [TheOneAJ](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/161373/TheOneAJ) for writing this somewhat religious story.

### [The Eternal Date](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571865/the-eternal-date)
Twilight's inability to let go is interesting. Instead of living a real life with her remaining friends, she chooses to live in a constructed world with a presumably fake Rarity re-making the same date every day. It's sad to see Twilight go so far and to hear about what she would have done if her friends had went along. I love the ship, its one of my favorites. The characters are great. Thank you [Not That Anon](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/386458/Not+That+Anon) for writing this eternal story.

### [Storm Chasing](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571439/storm-chasing)
Some ponies are like storms, and some ponies can't help but chase them. Rainbow Dash's insistence on being with Lightning is ruining her life whether she sees it or not. The element not recognizing her is a pretty dire situation. The small interludes about storm chasers were interesting. The pairing is cute, if not sad because of the ending. I liked this story despite the weird formatting. Thank you [LockandKeyHyena](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/388630/LockandKeyHyena) for writing this stormy story.

### [Diamond Ring](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571762/diamond-ring)
This story is tragic. I feel bad for Rarity and Luna. Rarity having been manipulated for five years, that's not something you can adjust to normality quickly from. Luna having just returned and with one of her saviors in love with her, while she herself doesn't remember any of it. The ship is interesting and I liked it. The characters were great. The ending is sad. Hopefully Rarity can return and get over her love… Thank you [gloamish](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/606398/gloamish) for writing this tragic story.
