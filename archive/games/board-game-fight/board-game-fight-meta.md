# Board Game fight

## Synopsis:
A game about fighting via board games instead of physical combat.

## Potential Games:
- card games
  - poker
  - blackjack
  - rummi
  - war
  - palace
- chess
- checkers
- dominos
- uno
- mancala
- backgammon
- scrabble
- connect 4
- go

## Ideas:
 - Dungeon crawler / rogue like.
 - Floors could be 3 mini games and 1 full game.
 - Upgrades and abilities are gained via new game pieces.
 - Pieces are found in the dungeon or bought from a shop.
 - Each unique piece will have a unique look.
 - Cheating could be allowed?
 - Each enemy type could have a board game they play.
 - Levels could either be a physical floor you traverse or just a set of enemies you face.
 - Every piece should be useful in more than 1 game.
 - 

## Game Piece Ideas:
- Different colored dice that skew the roll.
- Chess pieces that let you leave the board.
- Cards that imitate another card.
- Two color cards.
- Two suite cards.
