# Join The Herd!

## Synopsis:
A game about a herd of ponies migrating. You try and get as many ponies in your herd as you can. The more ponies you have the more money you can make.

## Ideas:
- Different ponies can do different things in the herd.
- Ponies can provide passive benefits to the herd.
- Each pony has a requirement that must be met for them to stay in the herd.
