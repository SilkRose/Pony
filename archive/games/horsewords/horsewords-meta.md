# Horsewords

## Synopsis:
A cross between Balatro and Scrabble. You type words to gain score and beat the goat.

## Ideas:
- It would be like Balatro and Scrabble, where you have to type words to score points.
- Terminal game?
- I was thinking it could have 3 score parts: letter, syllable, and multiplier.
- You could upgrade letter/syllable score's kinda like in Balatro with planet cards.
- I would want to add some pony in here, so I thought you can have ponies that increase/multiply the syllables/letters found in their name.
