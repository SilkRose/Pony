# Deternal

Tagged story: [Deternal](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/590397/deternal)

***

This story was written in February, but I didn't have a cover to post it for a while. I like how it turned out. I was surprised at how many dislikes it got, but didn't let it get me down too much.

A decent amount of the feedback said there needed to be more to the story. I never thought of adding more at the time of writing. The story was fueled by grief, a family friend of 20 years died at the start of this year, and later last year my grandfather died.

I didn't know how to handle this, so I wrote this story. This story was very personal to me, so it's hard to separate the negative feedback from the emotions it was written in.

Someone who looked at it and never finished it said it wasn't something I'd write. They even said it was weird to include romance in a story about someone's death, but they didn't know either person, and this story wasn't *about* the people, it was a response to an emotion.

I may one day write a story or two more directly dedicated to these people, but for now this story will have to do.
