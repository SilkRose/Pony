Tagged story: [Heartbeat Hotel](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/593999/heartbeat-hotel)

This story is my 50th published story on Fimfiction! I'm so happy to be apart of this community and get to write stories people love. I tried my best to make this one special, hopefully you think I succeeded.

Also, I have something to announce…

I'm now a site moderator on Fimfiction!

I've been a mod for about 5 days now. There's a lot to take in but I'm slowly getting the hang of it. I love this site and helping out.

Now, back to the story. I asked [RunicTreetops](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/489485/RunicTreetops) for help on this story, and he taught me a lot. I'm trying to implement the stuff I learned in future stories, but sometimes I forget.

The cover for this one was another amazing artwork by [Lossart](https://www.tumblr.com/blog/markiza297).

This story is different than a lot of my other romances, in this one they're already together and get to spend some quality time with each other. I think it's really sweet and cute.
