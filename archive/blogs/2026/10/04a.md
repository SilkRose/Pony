# Metal Magic

Tagged story: [Metal Magic](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/593558/metal-magic)

***

Sorry it's taken so long for this blog. Before I get into what I've been up to and talk about this story, I am still asking for donations. If you're interested in helping, please read my blog post [here](https://www.fimfiction.net/blog/1140447/fixfiction-development-fund-asking-for-donations) about FixFiction's future.

I've been doing a lot of writing recently. I'm trying to release a story every other week until the end of the year. I'm also planning to release one blog per week to catch up with the stories I haven't talked about yet.

I've also been commissioned by a fandom comic artist to create a comic uploading & management site. That's where most of my effort has gone in the last week. It's coming along nicely, almost to the end. Once it's done, I'll start work again on FixFiction.

Now onto the story!

This story was fun to write. Like the previous commission for Admiral Biscuit, I got to use my knowledge of metal and recycling here. This time however I had to do more research since I've never worked at a scrap yard.

The cover is actually a Minecraft build I made with the help of [Ashy](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/499793/ashley1227). We tried to make it look like it could be a painting from the game. It was built on the creative server for [Love & Tolerance](https://love-tolerance.com/). You can go on the server and find it if you wanted. You might also find a future cover build…

I'm happy with how it turned out. I loved talking about something I know a lot about and getting another chance to use Iron Oxide in another story! I'm also glad the recipient seemed to like it as well.

Thanks for reading, see you next week!
