# Pony Pyramid

Tagged story: [Pony Pyramid](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/594845/pony-pyramid)

***

Originally this story was going to be about the mane 6 arguing over which shape was best for their song and dance routine. The idea never went anywhere, and [Ashy](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/499793/ashley1227) thought of the idea for what it turned out to be.

[Shay492](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/840747/Shay492) ended up writing over half of this story, it wouldn't be nearly as good without her. They really took charge and helped me get back into writing at the time. She also wrote the short and long description. Thank you!

I asked Shay for anything they wanted to include in the blog and she said:
> This was a very fun story to work on. I loved the direction it ended up taking. I think Silk and I both came up with some very funny jokes. Plus, we got to flex our speedwriting muscles. This whole story was written out in just over three hours from outline to finished first draft! I look forward to the next story we write together ^-^

As for my thoughts on the story itself, I'm happy with how it turned out. I like how absurd it is. The cover is nice and simple.

Thanks for reading, see you next week with another story!
