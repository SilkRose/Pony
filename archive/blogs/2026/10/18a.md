# Front-Page Blues

Tagged story: [Front-Page Blues](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/594455/front-page-blues)

***

I really like how this story turned out. I love shipping these two! This story is somewhat similar to a previous story of mine: [Creative Freedom](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/548850/creative-freedom). This story is basically my original idea for that story. At the time we didn't want to make it a romance.

As someone pointed out in the [comments](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/594455/front-page-blues#comment/12499859), this story is about getting stories featured and the reactions to it. I had just released my 50th story, I had put a lot of effort into it and then it gets double digit down votes.

None of the comments on [Heartbeat Hotel](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/593999/heartbeat-hotel) were negative, so I don't know why this story got so many down votes. All I could do was write my emotions. I wrote this story entirely  before [Heartbeat Hotel](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/593999/heartbeat-hotel) left the feature box.

I really like how the cover turned out for this one. The Pinkie on the magazine and the Pinkie in the cover give me *don't talk to me or my son ever again* vibes and I love it. The background is a reference to something, but I won't say what.

Also, I am still asking for donations. If you're interested in helping, please read my blog post [here](https://www.fimfiction.net/blog/1140447/fixfiction-development-fund-asking-for-donations) about FixFiction's future.

Thanks for reading, hope you like the new story!
