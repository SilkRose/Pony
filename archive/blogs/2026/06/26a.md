# Fleetfall (Still Asking for Donations)

Tagged story: [Fleetfall](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/591113/fleetfall)

***

Before I get into this story, I want to mention my family and I still can use any donations you can give. In return for donations I am coding on FixFiction to try and make it better and add a bunch of new features. Thank you to anypony who donates or has in the past. To read more about this effort and how to donate, please read my last blog post [here](https://www.fimfiction.net/blog/1140447/fixfiction-development-fund-asking-for-donations).

My next blog is most likely going to be a dev-blog about all the work being done on FixFiction.

This story was a gift to somepony who missed out on participating in Jinglemas the first year I did it, in 2024. [Shakespearicles](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/83757/Shakespearicles) told me they liked Fleetfoot, so I came up with this idea. It just took me a while to finally write it…

I really like how this story turned out. [Math Spook](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/612387/Math+Spook) did an amazing job bringing the story together. I was stuck figuring out this one near the end. The original idea that this story came from was: Fleetfoot goes really high and free falls while she thinks and doesn't open her eyes until she resolves the problem.

I also really like the cover for this one. [Lossart](https://www.tumblr.com/blog/markiza297) did another amazing job here! [Scriblits Talo](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/495925/Scriblits+Talo/stories) made a cover for this one too, but I had it remade by Loss. You can view Scrib's version [here](https://github.com/SilkRose/Pony/blob/mane/stories/fleetfall/covers/fleetfall-concept-cover-1.jpg). I wanted something a bit more polished since it was a gift.

Thanks for reading!
