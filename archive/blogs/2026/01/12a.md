Tagged story: [The Tour of Twilight's Castle](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/586114/the-tour-of-twilights-castle)

The prompt is the same as the previous story, since it was for the same pony:
> This year I'd like to ask for, _shockingly_, more unicorn Sunset Shimmer. Don't care if the setting is the human world or Equestria, but Sunset needs to be her unicorn self. Story also don't have the be holiday themed or take place during the holidays; I'm fine with whatever.

My next story is done. It just needs a bit more proofreading and a cover. Ended up at 6.5K words.
