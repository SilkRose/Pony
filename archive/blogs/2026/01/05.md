# Afterlife Affection

Tagged story: [Afterlife Affection](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/586113/afterlife-affection)

***

This was the first of three stories I wrote for [Jinglemas](https://www.fimfiction.net/group/213263/jinglemas) 2025. The first two were released on Christmas, and were both for the same pony. I was told nopony had ever written their pony two stories before for this event. So, I decided to do it.

I've had this story idea for a while, and it fit the prompt, and I really wanted to do it. It is a sadder story, so I made sure the second story gift was a happy one.

The prompt I got was:
> This year I'd like to ask for, _shockingly_, more unicorn Sunset Shimmer. Don't care if the setting is the human world or Equestria, but Sunset needs to be her unicorn self. Story also don't have the be holiday themed or take place during the holidays; I'm fine with whatever.

This story went through a lot of changes. I originally had a twist ending where the two Pinkie Pie's were swapped at the time of death, but sadly I couldn't get it working. I'll just save that idea for another story. ![:raritywink:](../../../emotes/raritywink.png)

I like how this turned out, nice and sad. I love the cover, [Lossart](https://www.tumblr.com/blog/markiza297) did an amazing job!

I'm writing my next story, it's coming along great! I'm 2,664 words into it. It's going to be a long one I think. I'm very excited for it and hope you are too!

See you soon!
