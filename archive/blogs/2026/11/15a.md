Before I get into this story, I want to mention [Archive of Our Pone](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/596227/archive-of-our-pone). It's an ongoing collaboration story that celebrates the show and its characters. If you're a writer and you want to join this collaboration, please reach out to [Silk Rose](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/237915/Silk+Rose).

I've been wanting to write more comedy lately, so I'm glad I could write this story. I like how it turned out. Trying to write in a way that played off the typical depiction of hacking in movies while also using my real world coding knowledge was fun.

It was also a lot of fun to write the bloopers for this story. That's something I'd like to do for other stories in the future.
