# Letters of Lament

Tagged story: [Letters of Lament](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/575613/letters-of-lament)

***

I really liked how this story turned out. This was my second attempt at writing a story for April Fools. And even then I had to drop the original idea. I wanted the story to be about Pinkie getting banned from Twilight's library. I was going to call it Ban Appeal.

Both times I tried writing the story, it wound up better with the banning aspect removed. The second is what was published as Letters of Lament. The first has been renamed and will come out sometime later.

This story was released for April Fools, but nothing in the story was a joke or stunt, it was just a normal story. No, the thing I decided to do this year was release a story while banned from the site. Yep, I was banned for all of April 1st and still released a story.

As far as I'm aware, I'm the first and only person to ever do this. I won't explain how I did it, a magician never reveals their tricks. I also was specifically banned for this stunt, I didn't do anything malicious to get banned.

I'm planning on releasing another story this month, and I've started working on FixFiction again.

Thanks for reading!
