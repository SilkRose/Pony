# Chaotic Crush

Tagged story: [Chaotic Crush](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/570257/chaotic-crush)

***

This story was a lot of fun to write. It is the first time I've done a choose your own ending story. I'm glad [kleia](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/482079/kleia) liked it! The cover maze was very tedious to make. So far, only two people have solved it and gotten the prize. There's still one prize left!

This story changed how I felt about Fluttershy x Discord, before I didn't like it much, and now, it's still not my favorite, but I can definitely see the appeal. [Ashy](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/499793/ashley1227) helped write this one. This was her first foray into writing, and I think she did great!

This story was for [Jinglemas 2024](https://www.fimfiction.net/group/213263/jinglemas/thread/481574/jinglemas-2024), my first year participating. As for the story I got: [A Pink and Purple Practice Date Perhaps?](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/570155/a-pink-and-purple-practice-date-perhaps). I loved it! It went into my favorites. [vren55](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/26998/vren55) did an amazing job. It's very cute and sweet, I love the pairing, Pinkie x Rarity was the first ship I wrote with Pinkie, so it was awesome they chose Rarity.

The prompt I gave was as follows:
> I would the story to include Pinkie Pie and one other member of the mane 6. Preferably a romance, and if not a comedy. If those don't work, anything else is also fine. Ponies or EQG is fine.

The prompt I received was this:
> I'd love to see something centered around Fluttercord bc listen they're so cute. No particular prompt other then that, maybe some cutesy romance and fluff but otherwise, writer can go insane.

That's all for now, see you next time!
