# Hearth's Warming Homecoming

Tagged story: [Hearth's Warming Homecoming](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/571171/hearths-warming-homecoming)

***

This story is the one-year point of [PseudoBob's](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/12771/PseudoBob+Delightus) involvement in my stories. I'd like to personally thank him for all the help he gives me. He's a great friend, and deserves more recognition and followers! Please go check him out if you haven't already.

This story was written for [Test4Echo](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/117818/Test4Echo) for [Jinglemas 2024](https://www.fimfiction.net/group/213263/jinglemas/thread/481574/jinglemas-2024). It was a replacement story since the original one they got included romance.

The prompt I got was:
> For my story request, I was looking at a slice of life story (could have some light comedy in it, if so desired. I feel like I say that every year. ![:pinkiecrazy:](../../../emotes/pinkiecrazy.png)) with Sunset and Celestia. It can include a bit of EqG, but I would ask that whomever is assigned writes the majority of it taking place in Equestria.
>
> As long as there is no romance/shipping.

[Ashy](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/499793/ashley1227) did a lot of work on the cover. She basically finished it for me after I got it started. This story was my first time really writing Sunset or Celestia. Hopefully I did well.

I'm planning to release my next story on Wednesday, or maybe next Sunday. We shall see.
