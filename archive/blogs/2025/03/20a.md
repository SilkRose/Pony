# Considering Canon: What is and isn't Canonical?

When it comes to what is and isn't canon, many people seem to get this wrong in the pony community. I'm here to give my viewpoint and explain why I think my definition is the correct and only approach that should be valid.

Here is what constitutes my defining features of canon:
1. Everything officially licensed under the My Little Pony brand is canon.
2. Nothing said by show staff on social media is canon.

This definition lacks any nuance at all. The rest of this blog will be said missing nuance.

Before we get into the details, I want to explain why I am writing this blog. Simply put: everywhere I see, people are wrong about what is and isn't canon. Everyone says the comics aren't canon, when it's not their decision to make.

**If it's officially licensed, its officially canon.**

Unless you own the My Little Pony brand, you do not get to decide. You can consider the comics non-canon in your head, thus a head-canon, but you cannot say they are non-canon to the brand.

Let me start with the second point from above. Many show staff have said on social media things pertaining to the show and brand as a whole. These things are not canon.

**Unless it came from Hasbro, it is not canon.**

These social media posts can be considered fanon, or fandom-canon, but they are not canon because they were never shown in officially licensed media, or by Hasbro directly.

Now onto my first point.

You might have some questions about how everything licensed can be canon if some of it contradicts itself. That's easy, you just need to look at perspective.

**Canon is a tier list and the focal point is S.**

What I mean by this is, from the perspective of My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic, itself would be S tier on the canon list. While other forms of media might be lower.

I will give some examples.

If MLP:FIM is S tier, then Best Gift Ever, Rainbow Roadtrip, and the movie are also S tier.

**Same medium, same characters, same tier.**

If MLP:FIM is S tier, then Equestria Girls is A tier.

**Same medium, different characters, lower tier.**

If MLP:FIM is S tier, then the comics are A tier.

**Different medium, same characters, lower tier.**

You might be wondering though, if Equestria Girls and the comics are both A tier from the perspective of the show, which takes precedence?

Look at the medium in which they were produced.

**Medium settles disputes.**

The reason the movie and those two specials are S tier alongside the show is because they are the same medium, and the same characters.

Equestria Girls is the same medium, so therefore it is above the comics in canon.

With all that said, where does the perspective play into this?

Let's say we want to know the tier-list for Equestria Girls canon, different from the mane show examples above.

All the Equestria Girls movies, shorts and specials would be S tier canon.

The mane show (MLP:FIM) would be A tier.

The Equestria Girls comics would be A tier.

The mane show comics would be B tier.

The Equestria Girls comics are above the mane comics because they are the same characters.

**The more separation, the lower the tier.**

You might argue that they are the same characters between the mane show and Equestria Girls, but they are not, it's a different world.

But, what about when a future episode changes or retracts something shown in a previous episode?

**Perspective also works on a timeline.**

Lets say we want to know what the tier list might be for season 4 of the mane show.

Season 4 would be S tier.

Season's 3 and 5 would be A tier.

Season's 2 and 6 would be B tier.

Etc.

This perspective can get even more granular by going down to the episode level.

Tier-lists are by there very nature, a tiered system, so something in tier S outranks something in tier B.

If something in the comics goes against the mane show, the show takes precedence if that is your focal point. This doesn't mean that all comics are non-canon, it just means that one specific thing isn't canon to the show.

So, basically everything is canon.

The comics, generation 5, the collectible card game, the trading card game, the commercials and promotions, the toys, everything; it's all canon.

The perspective is what matters when it comes to determining what is and isn't canon to a specific piece of media.

If something in Season 9 contradicts something in Season 2, it doesn't matter if your only concern is Season 2.

I didn't write this blog to say you should care about canon in your writing. I constantly write mane 6 romance stories. I will never write Pinkie x Cheese, because to my head-canon, that's not real. No, I'm still not over it.

I want to know what you think of this tier-list style perspective on canon? I want to know what you think a tier-list for the show would be? Generation 5? Equestria girls?

I've had this idea of canon being a tier-list in my head for over a decade now, and I just had to make a blog and see what others think. If you read the whole thing, thank you! You all mean the world to me! <3
