# Pink Reflections

Tagged story: [Pink Reflections](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/579531/pink-reflections)

***

This story was originally a collaboration with [6-D Pegasus](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/293755/6-D+Pegasus). He wrote half of the first section, about seven hundred words. This is also why he paid for the cover. We wrote that first section back in 2023, then both of us just kind of left it out to dry. We took turns writing a bit going back and forth. Finally I had enough and finished it on my own with 6-D's permission.

The next paragraph contains spoilers for Pink Reflections, be warned.

I love the cover for this one. If you notice the mirror showcases the three acts of the story: one where her reflection is sad, two where her refection is gone, and three where her reflection is happy and back. I love how this story turned out and hope it lives up to the writing quality of [6-D Pegasus](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/293755/6-D+Pegasus).
