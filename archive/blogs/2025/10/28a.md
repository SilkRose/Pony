# Asking for Help (Donations & Commissions)

I'd like to ask for your help to get me out of debt. I currently owe about $450 and have very little to no income. I would prefer to do commissions to earn your money, but I won't say no to anyone willing to donate and help me out.

My family scraps metal for a living and lately we haven't been finding very much. I live with my parents rent and bill free for helping out. Normally I make $10 to $20 a week helping my dad take a load of metal to the junkyard, but lately we haven't made enough to pay me.

With all my accrued debt, the worsening scrap prospects, and the likely-hood of food-stamps not being paid out next month, I'm afraid I must ask for help for me and my family.

I know a few languages, from most to least experience: Rust, Typescript/JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Bash/shell scripting. I also have experience with SQLite and PostgreSQL.

I can code a lot of different thing, just contact me to see if I can do what you need.
