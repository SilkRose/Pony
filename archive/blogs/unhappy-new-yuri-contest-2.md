# (Un)Happy New Yuri Contest 2

This time around I am writing about each story as I judge them. I've been very busy with a coding project, but I must take a break from that to read ad judge some Yuri!

***

### [A desert of dreams.](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/585103/a-desert-of-dreams)
A unique pairing brought to life in great detail. I loved the sweet moments between Somnambula and Luna. I like stories that show events from all the way back in time, and bringing up generation 5 lore is a nice touch. I feel bad for how Luna gets treated in the end. Overall, this story was cute and sad. I really like the ending, despite its sad nature, and it caught me by surprise.  Thank you [Dumbwitch](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/854987/Dumbwitch) for writing this cool story.

### [Sweet Betrayal](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/585684/sweet-betrayal)
I concur, this story never happened. Such a beautifully written tail of deceit and passion. It captures Rarity's writing style wonderfully, darling. I'm a sucker for mane 6 ships, and this is no exception. If we are to assume this story never happened, which it didn't, then it's sad that Fluttershy and Rarity go on to live life loveless. However, on the other hoof, if this story did happen, which it didn't, then Rarity should probably see a therapist. Fluttershy too if she finds out… Thank you [Baal Bunny](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/176618/Baal+Bunny) for writing this deceitful story.

### [The Raven and the Clockmaker](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/585848/the-raven-and-the-clockmaker)
There's almost definitely some symbolism in this one that I'm not picking up. I still enjoyed this story, nonetheless.  I liked Twilight in this, sad all she went through. Twilight and Pinkie is my favorite ship. If my interpretation is correct, Twilight was chosen to save the word and is doing so by winding up a clock, to relive a world that ends up with her death. And Pinkie is choosing to doom the world? I don't know. Thank you [CatStar9](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/848424/CatStar9) for writing this symbolic story.

### [Second Chance](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/585864/second-chance)
Slow and methodical, this story tells itself with precision and grace. Full of character in every paragraph, this story gripped me from the start. The characters were on point and great. I felt a connection to Twilight while reading this with a headache, not of the story's fault. The pairing is one I don't think I've read, but was done was very believable. I'm glad Twilight got out and is going to hopefully live the life she deserves. Thank you [Revel Montaro](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/353633/Revel+Montaro) for writing this realistic story.
