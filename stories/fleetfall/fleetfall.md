# Fleetfall

Fleetfoot pumped her wings one last time, making it as far upward as she dared. She brought her wings in and lowered her goggles that had blinders on them. Beginning to fall, she felt the weight of the world was lifted off her shoulders.

*Alright, Fleetfoot. You can do this, just think. Think.*

*I love Rainbow Dash.*

Just the thought alone almost made her smile. Unfortunately for her, now she had to deal with the consequences of her own admission.

*I love Rainbow Dash, and I think she likes me back…*

*The reasons I like her: all of them. She's brave, beautiful, and my best friend.*

*If she did like me, why hasn't she asked me out? Rainbow Dash isn't the type of pony to hide how she feels. Usually, I'm not either.*

*I guess I can think of one reason: Wonderbolts aren't allowed to date.*

*While it hasn't stopped ponies in the past, we would have to keep it secret.*

She had felt the pressure shifting as the air rushed past her. She'd reached terminal velocity. Her mane and tail whipped in the violent free fall.

*What would a future with Rainbow Dash even look like?*

She imagined what it might be like to date Dash, each idea brought a brighter and brighter hue of red to her cheeks.

A sweet hug after a long day.

Their lips pressed together.

A romantic dinner in the city.

Holding each other in a serene peace.

Shaking the blush from her face, she changed her train of thought to what it would mean for their jobs.

*Hiding it wouldn't be the hardest. Some team members would never tell, but some might complain.*

*If we got caught…*

*I'd hate it if either of us lost our spot on the team.*

*Would they just make us break up? That sure would be preferable.*

*Rainbow Dash isn't always the most subtle pony at times.*

*She might be if her job depended on it.*

She shifted her thoughts to what a relationship would mean for them personally.

*We would spend more time together outside of work.*

*We would go on dates.*

*We might one day move in together.*

*We might get caught and fired.*

This thought dragged her down. She didn't know the future and could only try to think through it.

Refocusing on Rainbow Dash, she thought about how she felt towards her.

*Ever since she joined the bolts, I've been smitten with her.*

*I think she picked up on this and started having an interest in me.*

*She makes me feel great.*

*I can only hope that I do the same.*

*She makes me want to sing a song.*

*I can only hope that I do the same.*

*She makes me want to kiss her sight on scene.*

*I can only hope that I do the same.*

Her thoughts drifted to how she thought Rainbow Dash saw her.

*I think she likes me.*

*I think she's playing it cool, because she is.*

*The way she hugs me.*

*The way she stares at me.*

*The inklings of a blush on her face when she's around me.*

*It all makes me think she likes me.*

Before she can think of anything else, she was hit with a blast of cold and moisture as she crashed through a cloud.

*I need to speed this up…*

*What does it mean if I'm wrong and she doesn't like me?*

*Well, that would suck.*

*It would solve the whole problem, though.*

*Not exactly the outcome I'm looking for, but I need to be prepared for it anyway.*





*Time to go ask her out. For better or worse, I need to make my feelings known.*

She used her wings and started to slow down as she took the blinders off her goggles. Seeing the ground was quickly approaching, she turned sharply to try to stay airborne.

Barely missing the ground, she sighted in relief.

*That was the closest call this month, I need to be more careful doing this.*
