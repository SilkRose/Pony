# Buy my Butt

Pinkie bounced over to Rarity. "Rarity, I found a book about is! Want to read it?"

Rarity took the book in her magic. "Buy my Butt by Davenport. Pinkie, are you sure this is appropriate?"

Pinkie shrugged. "I didn't see anything bad when I looked at it."

Rarity opened the book and read to Pinkie…

> Rarity's head was gently resting on Pinkie's flanks. She was looking up at Pinkie with a smile.
>
> "Pinkie, your cutie mark is so soft, it makes the absolute best cushion."
>
> Pinkie blushed. "Aww, thank you."

Rarity asked, "Why do so many ponies write about us dating each other?"

Pinkie just shrugged.

> Rarity blushed to. "It's the perfect [pony pillow](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/541813/pony-pillows)."
>
> Pinkie frowned. "How can I experience my pillowy-ness? I can't get my head to lay on my flanks…"
>
> Rarity thought for a second. "How about I make you a cutie mark cushion. I can make a heart shaped cushion and compare it with your flanks to get it just right."
>
> Pinkie cheered. "Yay!"

Rarity said, "That's a cute idea."

Pinkie agreed. "Yep!"

> Rarity worked on Pinkie's cushion over the next several days. Each iteration feeling more accurate than the last. She kept the iterations secret until it was ready to give to Pinkie.
>
> The next time they were cuddling with the cushion completed, Rarity surprised Pinkie.
>
> "Pinkie, I have a present for you."
>
> Pinkie got excited. "Oh, what is it?"
>
> Rarity sat up and levitated a wrapped present to Pinkie.
>
> Pinkie opened it in a hurry with a smile. It was the cutie mark cushion promised before.
>
> Pinkie hugged the cushion, feeling how soft it was. It matched her coat perfectly, with her cutie mark stitched into the left lobe of the heart on one side, and the right on the other.

Pinkie blushed slightly in real life. "Rarity, I know were not dating, but can you make me a cutie mark cushion in real life?"

Rarity giggled. "Sure, I'm curious how it would turn out myself anyway…"

> "Its so soft!"
>
> Rarity blushed. "It matches you exactly."
>
> Pinkie blushed. "Thank you!"
>
> Rarity leaned in and Pinkie followed suit until their lips met. The kiss lasted until Pinkie had an idea…
>
> "Rarity!"
>
> She giggled. "What, darling?"
>
> Pinkie explained enthusiastically, "We should make more cutie mark cushions and sell them!"

Rarity quirked, "That would explain the name."
