# The Tragic Alicorn

Rainbow Dash rifled through a stack of stories. “Ugh,” she muttered to herself, “isn’t there a single good story in here somewhere?” She set the stack aside and reached for another.

A few looseleafed sheets of paper stuck out. “Hmm,” said Rainbow Dash. The sheets, which had been ripped from a notebook, had tattered edges, and the pencilwork on them was sloppy. “‘The Tragic Alicorn.’ By Anonymous.” She shrugged. “Could be worse, I guess.”

---

Once upon a time or something like that. Rainbow Dash was super fast, like way, way faster than anypony else. All the time, ponies came up to her and were like, “I bet I can beat you in a race,” and she just said, “Ha!” and then she sonic rainboomed them and the rainboom was so strong they fell out of the sky. But Rainbow Dash was so fast that she could catch them, and they’d say, “Rainbow Dash! Thanks for saving me! You’re the greatest!” This happened ten times every day.

One time Rainbow Dash was flying. And a ~~mysterius~~ ~~mysteerios~~ ~~misterius~~ mistireeus alicorn flew up to her. Her coat was all black, and her mane and tail were red like blood. She was super buff and her wings were giant. Rainbow Dash looked at her and thought, “Who is this strange alicorn flying next to me?”

The alicorn said, “I bet I can beat you in a race.” Rainbow Dash knew what to do, so she sonic rainboomed. But the alicorn did a double sonic rainboom! The power of the rainbooms made everything around them explode, and all the ponies who saw it were like, “Wow, this mistireeus alicorn is really strong.”

Rainbow Dash asked, “Who are you?”

The mistireeus alicorn tossed her mane. She made it look so cool that everypony except Rainbow Dash was jealous. But Rainbow Dash was already too cool to be jealous. “I had a terrible, tragic childhood that scared me for life,” said the alicorn.

“I really want to know who you are.”

But the alicorn started crying and then flew away. Because she was scared for life even though she thought she got over it.

So Rainbow Dash went searching for the mistireeus alicorn. She found her in a cave crying. The cave was dark like the alicorn's feelings.

“What’s wrong?” asked Rainbow Dash.

“I’m so lonely,” said the alicorn. “My parents never loved me. I never had anypony take care of me growing up.”

“Do you want a friend?”

“No! Everypony says they want to be my friend. But they just see an alicorn, they don’t care about me. I can’t have friends because I’m too tragic.”

“I want to be a real friend. What’s your name?”

The alicorn cried harder. “Nopony ever asked my name before! I’m Speedadee!”

Now the alicorn wasn’t mistireeus anymore. Rainbow Dash gave Speedadee a big hug. “Let’s be friends, Speedadee.”

Speedadee stopped crying. “You’re the greatest, Rainbow Dash.”

---

Rainbow Dash whistled softly to herself. “Okay. That was a story. I might need to go talk to someone later. Maybe sooner rather than later.”
