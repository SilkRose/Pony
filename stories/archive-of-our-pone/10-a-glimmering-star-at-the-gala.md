# A Glimmering Star at the Gala

"What's this?" Starlight asked, picking up an unusual book with a sparkly-looking cover off the mail-covered table. "A Glimmering Star at the Gala?"

"Huh?" Rarity peeked over her shoulder. "Oh, goodness! Just look at the *sparkles* on that thing! Who designed such an aesthetically-looking *masterpiece*?"

"It says here… *Sunburst*?" Her eyes widened briefly. "Huh. I didn't think he was a *writer* type of pony."

"Well," Rarity mustered, tapping a hoof against her muzzle. "He's quite the creative soul, dear. Shall we take a look?"

"I suppose so. You've gotten me quite curious, Rarity."

***

That time of the year had rolled around once more, and the Grand Galloping Gala was in preparation to bring forth the best night of everypony's lives. Celestia sent out letters from her cozy room, staring unto the starry night sky as each letter carried the promises of a lifetime, and a wish come true.

"Girls, girls!" Twilight exclaimed, setting a sealed scroll on the dining table with a resounding thwack. "Princess Celestia invited us to attend the Grand Galloping Gala this year!"

"Really? That's *awesome*!" Rainbow Dash added, a wide grin plastered on her face. "I can't wait to fly with the *Wonderbolts* for the opening ceremony! It's going to be *so* cool!"

The loud discussion at the dinner table turned to a cacophony of laughter and joy. While the Mane Six were happily planning their dresses, two pairs of eyes watched them, sharing a look of mutual uncertainty.

***

Later that evening, the same two pairs of eyes sat down at the table, opposite of each other.

"I don't understand, Spike," Starlight murmured, looking around as if expecting somepony to eavesdrop. "They want me to tag along, but I don't have a dress. How can I go without an attire?"

"I don't think it's about the attire, Starlight," Spike added, tapping his claw against the table. "Maybe they want you to come along since you're their friend?"

"But we haven't even known each other for that long!"

"No matter! I think we could get you something—"

"No, Spike." Starlight let out a deep sigh, holding back the wetness that pricked in the corners of her eyes. "I… I'm not coming. I'm just not part of the *team*."

Spike opened his mouth, but no words came out. Starlight stood up and turned towards the door, the seat briefly creating a loud, dragging sound as she moved away from the table.

Starlight turned back to look at him. "I'm really sorry, Spike. No harsh feelings?"

"No harsh feelings…" he muttered, the words leaving behind a bitter taste.

As Starlight walked out of the dining room, Rarity walked in. She moved with a measured stride, deliberately leaning against the chair as she dramatically posed in front of Spike. "Goodness, dear," she said, huffing in uncertainty. "Whatever happened between you two? Her eyes looked like waterfalls!"

"Well, I tried to tell her but—"

"But what, dear?"

"She won't tag along for the Grand Galloping Gala, and she's on her way to tell Twilight."

"WHAT?!" Rarity gasped. "Oh no, no. This won't do. Why, dear, was she so upset about the Gala?"

"She doesn't exactly have a *Gala* dress," he added, wetting his lips. "So she doesn't believe she is part of the *team*."

"Nonsense, darling!" Rarity straightened herself, dismissively waving her hoof. "I shall make her the *prettiest* dress in all of Equestria! No good friend of Twilight is going to end up alone on such a charming occasion!"

"Then we'd better go tell Twilight," Spike added, jumping off the seat and turning towards the door. "And we better move fast, before she gets any ideas about writing Celestia a five-page letter on why Starlight didn't want to come."

***

Over the coming days, Rarity and Twilight coordinated their efforts to prepare the best gift ever for the one mare that felt like she deserved none. Starlight's letter of refusal never reached Celestia, nor was she aware that Twilight had buried the letter in her bedroom drawer, under a pile of old scrolls.

As three nights remained before the Gala, Twilight and Rarity nodded in unison, their plan set in stone. Spike wandered down the hallway, tiptoeing towards Starlight's room so as not to awake the others in the dead of the night.

A series of gentle knocks echoed against the door. "Psst, Starlight? Starlight, are you there?"

Spike waited outside for a minute. Then another. Then, the door opened.

"What?" Starlight mumbled, rubbing her eyes. "What is it? It's the middle of the—"

"I know, I know— Just come quick, please. It's an emergency!"

Her eyes widened in alarm and her back immediately straightened. "An… an emergency? Okay, okay."

As they walked down the hallway, Starlight yawned several times. Her muscles felt unusually stiff, and her magic lit up as she tiptoed alongside Spike.

"Where's the emergency? What's happening?" she asked, her voice laden with exhaustion. "Ugh… I just hope Twilight is safe."

"Well, um… Twilight needs your help, yes," Spike whispered, a bead of sweat trailing down his face.

"Are you sure? You seem… *nervous*."

Before she could question him any further, they had reached the dining room. A series of hushed, yet audible murmurs echoed from the other side of the room. When Spike knocked on the door, the murmurs turned to shushes, before turning to silence.

"This is… *weird*," Starlight muttered, rubbing the back of her mane. "Are you sure this isn't a prank? Because if this is a prank—"

The door opened slightly, revealing a familiar face.

Twilight's voice echoed through the crack, a dead-serious expression on her face. "Starlight, we need to talk."

Starlight's heart sunk and her eyes widened, shaken by her unusually serious tone. "T–Twilight? What do you—"

Before she could say anything further, the door opened wide as Twilight pulled her inside.

Starlight yelped loudly as the momentum carried her stumbling inside. As she caught herself against a pillar, she turned towards Twilight. "Twilight, what was that for?!"

"Sorry, Starlight," Twilight sighed, her serious demeanor falling apart as a wide grin took over. "But I have a surprise for you."

"We all do, actually!" Rarity shouted, stepping out from behind a pillar with a moving rack filled with dresses. "Surprise, darling!"

Starlight blinked in confusion, tapping a hoof against her muzzle. "You… you woke me up and brought me here just to show off the *Gala dresses*?"

"What, *these*?" Rarity scoffed, fanning herself as her magic moved the dresses to the side, one at a time. "Don't be ridiculous, Starlight! I've overheard your little… *incident* with Spike, and I just couldn't let it slide!"

"*What*?" Starlight blinked twice. "What do you—"

A loud gasp escaped her as she stared dead-ahead. Rarity had moved all the dresses from the rack, save for one. The last dress on the rack stood out like a sore thumb, carrying the themes of not one, not two — but *all* of Starlight's personality. It was as if Rarity had sewn a copy of her onto the dress.

"I— I— I—" she stuttered, her mouth wide open. She was frozen in a gasp, yet she wanted to explode from joy. "Oh, oh— Oh my *Celestia*! Rarity, you… I—" She paused, turning towards Twilight. "But Twilight, the letter—"

"I never sent it, Starlight." She smiled, patting her on the shoulder. "Go on, try the dress!"

She didn't have to be told twice. Starlight practically appeared next to Rarity and the dress, startling the fashionista as she took her help in getting dressed.

…

"How do I look, Twilight?" she asked, tears streaming down her cheeks as a wide smile formed on her face.

"Don't ask her, dear!" Rarity huffed, tightening the last zipper on her back. "The stars themselves shall envy your beauty, Starlight!"

"I… thank you, Rarity. Thank you, thank you so much!" Starlight exclaimed, pulling her into a warm, tight embrace. "I don't want to miss out on the Gala anymore."

***

"Okay, you win," Starlight huffed, a smile reaching one corner of her mouth. "That was quite an emotional rollercoaster."

"Oh, Sunburst has *definitely* inspired me to make you the *dress of all dresses*!" she gushed, a brilliant spark in her eyes. "Though, if one must ask — how come you haven't asked me for a *Gala dress* yet?"

"Umm…"

"Nonsense, Starlight! Come on, let's go make you a dress a mare can only *dream* of!"
