# Rescue From the Forest of Darkness

Fluttershy pawed and nosed her way through one of the smaller piles. So many books, so many of them with *her* as a character… The idea made her wings twitch. Part of her didn’t even want to open a book, for fear it would be about her. In any way, shape or form.

But she couldn’t delay forever, and she eventually pulled out an unassuming title. *Rescue From the Forest of Darkness*, by… Cinder Shower. She cracked it open.

> Fluttershy

(She managed to keep reading.)

> swept back her mane, sending her shining, clear locks cascading through the air

Fluttershy managed to pull some of the tips of her mane into view. They needed a comb and working in the sanctuary had left them a bit dirty.

> and spread open her wings, revealing flawless feathers.

She’d been *meaning* to do some proper preening *eventually*, and now even a book was calling her out on it?

> Before her spread the Forest of Darkness. A foal had gotten lost inside! But ponies were too afraid to go inside, for the Forest of Darkness was the most hostile place in all the world. All sorts of strange creatures roamed beneath its boughs, creatures held back only by the light of Celestia’s sun, creatures nopony had seen before, creatures that could eat them up in a single bite.
>
> But Fluttershy. Well. She knew her creatures.
>
> Turning to the crowd behind her, she smiled warmly. “Fear not!” she said sonorously. ”I shall be back here ere sundown with your child intact! Away I go!” And she strode off into the dark trees.

“That’s not my voice,” she whispered. “That’s not my voice in two different ways.”

> She’d barely set a foot in the Forest before the eyes came. All sorts of colors and shapes loomed in the gloom before her, each pair wanting a piece of her. A fleshy piece. For these were the eyes of apex predators.
>
> And Fluttershy knew that apex predators were unused to their prey fighting back. So she glared right back at them and declared, “Don’t you think you can take me! I have a pony to rescue and I’m not going to let the likes of *you* stop me! For I am Fluttershy the great and noble, and nothing can stand in my way!”
>
> Even if they didn’t understand the words, the message was clear. One by one, the unseen creatures retreated until Fluttershy was alone again. She nodded to herself, rustled her wings, and strutted onwards.

This wasn’t her. She wasn’t this… paragonic. Why would anypony-

“You alright, Fluttershy?” Applejack asked, making Fluttershy twitch. “You’re squirmin’ somethin’ fierce.”

“Um… well…” Fluttershy flexed her wings. “This book, I’m in it, and it makes me look really… not-me.”

“How so? Wait, is it a kirin book?” asked Applejack. She leaned over to take a look at the page. It was only a moment before she confirmed, “Kirin book.”

“Um…”

“The kirins’re real grateful for what you and me did for ’em,” Applejack said. “Think the world of us. And I guess some of ’em show it differently than others. Can’t… really say I *like* it, but it is what it is.” She grinned and said, “You oughta see what some of ’em’ve written about *me*.”

Fluttershy looked at Applejack, then at the page, with its grand, heroic Fluttershy. Maybe, if that was how they really thought about her-

“I can take over if you want,” said Applejack.

“No, thanks,” Fluttershy said, surprising herself. “I’m- fine. Really.” Deep breath in, and she kept reading.

> Time and again, they came. Time and again, Fluttershy nobly held them back. She followed the foal’s trail onwards, through the gloom and silence.
>
> Then she heard small sobs, and she sped into a gallop. There the foal was, in a lucky clearing, sunlight forming a ring of light around him to keep the monsters away.
>
> “There you are!” proclaimed Fluttershy.
>
> At the sound of another voice, the foal perked up. The moment he saw who it was, all fear vanished. “Fluttershy!” he squeaked. “I knew you would come for me! You’re the only one brave enough!”
>
> Fluttershy pounced on him, forming a shield around him with her gleaming feathers. “Don’t worry, little one,” she declared, holding him tight. “We’ll get you home.” She spread her powerful wings wide and rocketed out through the canopy.
>
> In no time at all, she was landing outside the Forest, with the crowd cheering her. She landed, letting the foal run back to his family, smiling and waving at the adoring crowd. But she was humble. “Anypony who could speak to creatures like me would’ve done it,” she said bashfully. And then she was off. There were other ponies to help, all across Equestria, and she couldn’t bear to make them wait even a second longer. For she…
>
> …was *Fluttershy*.

Maybe a bit over-the-top, but if some people really felt she was that much of a hero… That feeling was nice. It was nice indeed.

Fluttershy pulled her mane into view again. Maybe she could clean it a bit better this time. Just this once.
