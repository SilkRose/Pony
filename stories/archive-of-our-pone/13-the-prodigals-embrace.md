## The Prodigal's Embrace

### Chapter 1

It was a dark and stormy night in Canterlot, the peak occasionally catching light from the stray lightning and thunder. Yet while it was a night most would choose to stay inside with a warm cup of tea and a good book, Twilight Sparkle had other obligations. Her cloak and saddlebags were thoroughly soaked, but she'd not ignore the message she'd gotten from her mentor and close friend.

Before much longer she reached her destination: The Headless Stallion. A deprecated tavern near the lower gates of Canterlot, one that had long since abandoned, being unable to compete with bigger and fancier places. It wasn't the first time Twilight had gone there, it was one of the few meeting places they'd rotate between.

But usually not for nights like these.

Making her way inside, Twilight briefly stopped to shake off the worst of the rain. She was barely halfway across the room before a hoarse voice spoke up.

"Did anypony follow you?"

Twilight gasped. "No, of course not. What happened to you? Where are you?" Her eyes scanned the room, but to no avail.

A few coughs came out, before they spoke up again. "Behind the bar."

Walking around the bar desk revealed to Twilight a harsh sight: On the floor lay her close friend and mentor in magic, Sunset Shimmer. Her own black cloak damp from the rain and… there was a large rip in her cloak? Tracing it revealed a sharp gash in her barrel. Twilight's mind grew blank as she stared.

Sunset chuckled. "Sad sight, isn't it?"

Shaking her head briefly, Twilight rushed over. "What happened?!?"

"She finally found out."

"Who? And about what?"

"Who else? And about my—" Sunset stopped to cough "—magic research."

Twilight could feel a chill run down her spine. "But weren't you-"

"-Looking into my special talent? Yes."

Sunset grinned from the floor. "I now know why I don't remember getting it. Or much of my foalhood for that matter."

Twilight grit her teeth as she worked a healing spell towards her friend's wound; she'd always been better at healing magic between the two of them. "Memory erasure?"

Sunset nodded. "It connects all the dots."

Having finished the spell, Twilight reached out with her hoof and helped Sunset up from the floor.

"What now?"

Sunset closed her eyes and sighed. "I'll need to go into hiding, there's nothing else to it."

Twilight awkwardly rubbed her front hoof against the other. "Are they that powerful? …Will I even get to see you again?"

Sunset flashed an apologetic smile. "It might be years. Which is why," she took a step closer to Twilight and gently nuzzled her cheek. "I'm gonna have to be honest with you."

Twilight could feel her heart beat increase.

"Something about you… something about your perfect student image always drove me in. The way you'd walk down the hall on campus, ignorant of the sneers ponies would throw your way because you were so far beyond them. The way you'd light up when we discuss high level magic concepts. The way you'd smirk when correcting me about that alchemy question from advanced alchemy three."

Sunset sighed and took a step back. "Somewhere along the line, Sparkle, I fell for you. And I can't depart before letting you know just how much."

Twilight gaped as her eyes dilated, yet she had no time to react as Sunset dove forwards and connected their lips.

It wasn't a perfect kiss, the two scholars were both novices in the language of love. And yet the passion and feelings between the two made it one they'd both remember for the rest of their lives.

When they finally parted, Twilight started sputtering. "But I thought! I thought you didn't like me that way!"

Sunset's eyes traced the floor. "I hadn't quite figured myself out back then, Twilight. I thought I had to find a nice stallion to have foals with." Sunset shook her head. "But I know now that it will never come to be. Both because of what I am…. but also because of my love for you, I have to be honest!"

Twilight took a step back, not quite knowing how to feel. She'd carried these emotions for Sunset locked up in a chest at the depths of her mind for so long now, knowing that they could _never_ be allowed to come out. Yet in but a few moments Sunset had all but shattered her mental fortitude.

And now standing there, tender eyes looking into one and another, she could be fully honest with herself: She was fully and completely in love with Sunset Shimmer.

Of course, it was at that moment that every window still whole shattered inwards.

"Tsk," Sunset let out before leaning in and whispering. "You still know that invisibility spell that you used to use to sneak around the archives in the after hours, right?"

Twilight nodded.

"Good, use it… and Twilight?"

"Yeah…?"

"Take care of yourself," Sunset finished, flashing Twilight her that smirk that always made her heart beat faster.

Sunset quickly walked out, with Twilight following suit under her invisibility spell. Outside the square was lined with the Royal Guard's elite. And towering behind them was none other than Princess Celestia herself. Twilight didn't quite know what to think.

Sunset snorted. "Came to finish the job yourself?"

Celestia briefly raised her wings. "I came to offer you another chance, Sunset. You've been my student for years, don't throw it all away on a foalish chase. Come back with me, please!"

Yet the pleading ran off of Sunset just the same as the pouring rain. "Come back with you. Why? So you can erase my memories _again_?" Sunset shook her head. "I walk my own path."

"Then you leave me no choice," Celestia said, signaling to her guards to take aim with their spears and crossbows.

"So that's it?" Sunset started, the confidence in her tone rising. "I don't follow heel, so now you'll do with me just like you did to your sister… Mother!"

The lightning bolt that flashed across the sky lit up the square and masked the sound of Twilight's gasp. Princess Celestia was Sunset's mother?!? It made little sense, yet the more she thought about it the more sense it did. The two of them had always had a weird relationship with one and another. More than student and teacher, yet less than family.

Celestia stared down at her child with contempt. "Your inherited connection to the sun is dangerous, surely you must see that!"

"You can't control everything, Celestia! Maybe one day that lesson will finally stick. But on this day—" Sunset lit her horn "—the lesson you'll learn: Is to never underestimate Sunset Shimmer!" A spell unlike any other went off as the guards charged her, one that turned Sunset's entire body into a ball of plasma that went flying off and out, away from Canterlot.

Celestia didn't hesitate, immediately taking to the air using her oversized wings. Yet Sunset had planned for that, Twilight realized. Because where Sunset had stood in the square remained her saddlebags, filled to the brim with fireworks, their fuse perfectly going off just as Celestia flew over where Sunset had once stood.

And yet… Twilight's eyes were not on her Princess, but rather on the love of her life flying off towards the horizon.

She didn't know it at the time, but that would be the last time she'd see Sunset Shimmer for years.
