# A Historical Retelling

“I’m confused,” Twilight muttered.

“What is it, Twi?” Applejack asked as she looked up from the transcript she’d been reading.

“This story is just a one-to-one retelling of the time we defeated Tirek! At least, that’s all it’s been so far.”

“I don’t think there’s anything strange about a historical retellin’. Heck, without her books, ol’ Goldie wouldn’t remember half our names!”

“I suppose I’ve just grown used to these stories being more… out there.”

“Why not read the ending out loud?” Pinkie excitedly chimed in. “It sounds like you’re getting to the best part!”

“Hmhm! Sure, Pinkie.”

***

“You're wrong, Tirek! I may have given you my Alicorn magic, but I carry within me the most powerful magic of all!”

And with those words, strands of light shot from the glowing bodies of Princess Twilight and her friends. They struck the tyrant one after another, each sapping more and more of his strength. His fate was sealed, and he was doomed to be locked away in Tartarus yet again.

But their plan did not work. With the last of his strength, Tirek ripped the light from his body, snapping the strands like they were mere twigs. His form grew large once more, and there was little the princess and her friends could do but gasp as he shot their magic back at them.

Then, out of the blue, a savior appeared! His beautiful blonde mane billowed in the wind! His chiseled abs glistened in the sunlight! His sharp, manly jawline made Rainbow Dash faint on the spot!

It was Zephyr Breeze, the TRUE hero of Equestria! 

He descended upon Tirek like he was some sort of Horse Jesus, the sheer power of his presence forcing the centaur to cower in fear! Even as Tirek fell to the ground and wept and perhaps wet himself a little, Zephyr showed him no mercy! One mighty kick was all it took to end the centaur for good, but the shining beacon of testosterone threw in a few extra punches just because he felt like it!

***

“...”

“Gee, I wonder who wrote this.”

***

The princess and her friends all fawned over their savior. He didn’t bask in the glory, of course, for he was much too humble. Naturally that didn’t stop Celestia, Luna, or even Cadance from declaring their undying love once he ripped them free of their bindings, love that he was all too happy to accept. Within hours he was sitting on a new throne in Canterlot Castle, way bigger and more comfortable than the old ones.

This was no less than he deserved, as I am sure any sane pony would agree. From that day forward, he ruled with grace and mercy, his several wives (including Rainbow Dash!) just as happy as he was. The only rule he signed into law, other than the obvious ones like “no committing crimes” (seriously, how did one else think of that before him?) was that he would be treated fairly for once in his life by the fairer sex. A contentious rule, perhaps, but one made in the name of prosperity. It made for a new dawn in Equestria, one where the gender roles were finally reversed for him. A Reverse Gender Roles Zephyr type of new dawn, an RGRZ, if you will.

And it was glorious.

The End.

***

The final two words had barely passed Twilight’s lips before Rainbow Dash grabbed the transcript from her hooves and, in one swift movement, tore it in half and swallowed it.

“R-Rainbow Dash!”

“I don’t support banning books,” she growled, “but I do support banning THAT.”

“It was rather… erm, embellished.”

“I, um,” Fluttershy mumbled, “think I’m with Rainbow Dash on this one.”

“Let’s never talk about that again,” Applejack groaned.

Twilight sighed.

“Yeah, fair enough.”
