# Albuckurque Neigh Mexicolt

“Twilight! Twilight! I found another book to read!” Pinkie Pie promptly pronked perfunctorily preforming partial pirouettes precisely parting ponies packed betwixt herself and her friend. “Hoochie mama what a mouthful!” Pinkie exclaimed to nopony in particular while tilting her head at an odd angle. The other ponies in the library as well as Twilight herself just stared nonplussed at the pink party pony until they collectively rolled their eyes and shook their heads with a smile.

“Anyhoozeles, Twilight! I was looking through that big pile of books and then my colon felt implodey, and my heart and spine felt a bit too close to each other for a moment and I got a craving for cabbage for four point thirteen seconds and my appendix felt like it was in danger when I picked up this book which is a combo I’ve never felt before but it was almost like a doozie but not quite but I knew you’d want to read it because it’s super interesting and I know you like interesting books so I brought it over immediately so here!”

Not even phased at this point, more so just curious, Twilight took the book in question within her raspberry magic aura before reading the cover. “Albuckurque, by Cheese Sandwich? Well this should be interesting if nothing else.”

Pinkie gasped dramatically. “Maybe it’s about the one nostriled scoundrel who goes around stealing glow in the dark lucky autographed snorkels Cheesie told me about!”

That got a pause out of Twilight before she sighed. “Well, according to the summery on the back, this is the anti-unauthorized abridged autobiography so I’m sure we’ll find out by reading.” Twilight floated the book back to her friend. “Would you care to do the honors Pinkie?”

“Would I!?”

Twilight cocked an eyebrow. “Would you?”

“Would I?!”

Twilight scrunched her face in confusion. “Would… you?”

Pinkie blinked. “Would I what?”

Twilight’s left eye twitched. “Would you like to read the book?”

Pinkie gasped, “Would I!”

Letting out a groan of exasperation, Twilight just shoved the book at Pinkie before loafing herself on a nearby sofa like a grumpy cat.

Pinkie gave a sheepish grin before settling in and loafing next to Twilight so she could read along. “Ahem,” Pinkie cleared her throat before some slightly upbeat and repetitive instrumentals began playing in the background. Twilight was confused about who was starting a heart song at a time like this before Pinkie’s singing voice began to narrate.

>Way back when I was just a tiny winy little wittle itty bitty colt living in a cider crate behind my mama’s house a hop skip and a jump away from Bobble Beery’s sweet shop? You know the place. Anyway back then my life was glitteriffic… except for the fact every single morning my mom would make me a big old bowl of sour grass for breakfast. It drove me insane.
>
> So one morning I asked her, “Hey mom! Why are you so obsessed with sour grass?”
>
> And my dear sweet mother Stick-In-The-Mud just looked at me like she always does and said in her usual monotone voice, "Technically, it’s good for you…” Then she tied me to a wall and fed me nothing but sour grass until I was twenty seven and three quarters years old.

“Well that’s an unsettling start,” Twilight quipped before the background music drowned her out again.

> That’s when I vowed that I would not stop, I would not rest, I would not sleep for an instant until the one nostriled stallion who may or may not be my deadbeat father was brought to justice. But just then a pimped out carriage rolled up to my house while I was mowing my lawn. A window opened and I could see a blue pegasus with a rainbow mane, a Pink earth pony with a Pink mane, and a white unicorn mare with a sickly green mane. They were the coolest ponies I’d ever seen.

A needle scratch was heard by all ponies in the room. “Wait, what?!” came a shrill scream from Rarity. “Setting aside all historical inaccuracies, why would Cheese describe Rainbow, Pinkie, and myself only to claim I had such a horrid coiffure!”

Pinkie pipped up. “Well Rares, technically he didn’t give any names so maybe it isn’t you?”

“What other white unicorns are there who associate with ponies matching the descriptions given? Rainbow Dash and her father are the only ponies I know of who possess such vivid manes and you are the pinkest pony there is!” Rarity stomped a hoof before pulling a couch from… somewhere and plopping onto it daintily before rearranging herself to fit the theme of loafs. “Well, do go on Darling. I must know of any more insults to my pride. I shall listen attentively.”

“Oki-Doki-Loki.” As Pinkie returned her attention, the ambient background music started up again.

> Sadly, I knew in my heart I was just too weird and nerdy for them. They took one look at me smiling and waving like some kind of princess and just kept going on their way and I never saw them again… or did I?
>
> Anyway it wasn’t long until I got hired as a brain surgeon. Turns out the local hospital was super low on staff and I looked just like one of the doctors so somepony yanked me off the street, scrubbed me down and plopped me in the operating room. Like that, I was a surgeon, cutting for the very first time. I wasn’t sure but I figured as long as I was there I would give it a shot.
>
> “Scalpel?”
>
> “Yes doctor!”
>
> “Forceps?”
>
> “Right away Doctor.”
>
> “Sponge?”
>
> “He-”
>
> “Bigger sponge dang it!”
>
> “So sorry! Right away Doctor!”
>
> “And somepony get me a rubber chicken STAT!”
>
> And that’s how I met my best buddy Boneless and performed brain surgery. I heard when the patient woke up they were barking mad but hey, we all have a little screw loose now and then.

“Wait, did he just admit to multiple felloneighs? Impersonating a doctor, preforming surgery without a license, possible identity theft, possible actual theft if he didn’t pay for any of that medical equipment and… Is that really how he found his first Boneless?” Twilight was taking furious notes only to be once again cut off by the story and accompanying background music.

> Okay so this one time I was running around town like a constipated Winona dog when I ran into the mare of my dreams. Her name was Pinkie. She was a Party enthusiast with a slight overbite and a mane the color of strange peaches. I’ll never forget the first thing she said to me. She said, “Hi, I’m Pinkie Pie! Also, everything you know is wrong! White is black, down is up, and long is short!”

Twilight and Rarity looked in shock to their party enthusiast who gave a helpless shrug. “What? It could be any Pinkie Pie!”

> We were inseparable after that! We ate together, we bathed Gummy the Alligator together, the world was our Purrito! So we got married and got us a house and had two beautiful foals, Li’l Cheese and Super Fly! But one faithful night, Pinkie asked me, she said, “Sweet Cheese? Do you wanna go to the drive through?”
>
> Ice in my veins. I had war flashbacks. I couldn’t do it again, not again. I refused to suffer the same fate again! “They. Forgot. THE ONIONS!” So we broke up and I never saw her again but that’s just the way things go… IN AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAALLBUCKURRRQUEEEE!

The stares returned. By this point Fluttershy and Rainbow Dash had joined the group, the latter giving her pink friend an incredulous stare while snuggling with Rarity. “What the hay Pinks! When did you get married?”

Pinkie gave a worried glance around at her friends. “But Dashie, I’m still a single pringle! I’m telling you galls either there’s another Pinkie out there or Cheese is just making this up! I don’t even like him like that.”

“How did he know about Winona and Gummy?” Fluttershy’s question went unheard and unanswered from her position on some floor cushions as the music cut off yet another pony and Pinkie continued the sordid tale.

> As luck would have it, it wasn’t long until I got the career of my dreams! That’s right I got a part time job at The Hay-Burger! I even made employee of the month after dealing with that purple unicorn slob. Oh yeah everypony was real jealous of me after that. I was getting a lot of attitude! Ya feel me?

Twilight’s mane started spazzing out as her right eye twitched. “No comment.” Twilight Purple Book Smart Horse Sparkle was learning.

Pinkie frowned and muttered under her breath, “This author and Cheese aren’t being very nice to my friends.” She eyed the 4th wall with suspicion before diving back into her task at hoof.

> So like this one time, at band camp, I saw this mare Applejack trying to carry a bucket of apples up some stairs by herself. So I said, “Hey, you want some help with that?”
>
> And Applejack just rolls her eyes and says, “No I want you to stick all four of my hooves together with melted cheese!” So I did.
>
> Then she gets all indignant on me! She was like “Hey partner, I was just being sarcastic!” Well that’s just great, how was I supposed to know that? Besides, now she’s got a really cute nickname, ‘Cheese Legs’!

Applejack finally joined the group as everypony looked to her for confirmation or rejection of these alleged events. “That no good varmint! Tha one tahm ah use that fancy city folk sarcasm and ah get tha one pony who shoots straighter than a pig on a truffle hunt. Mah hooves were sticky for weeks after that!”

Everypony, even Pinkie Pie is taken aback by that as Applejack casually joined Fluttershy in loafing on the floor pillows. As the background music started up again, the ponies sensed something foreboding on the horizon.

> Say, that reminds me of a little anecdote. This one pony came up to me on the street and said, “I haven’t had a bite in three days.”
>
> Well I knew what she meant, but just to be funny, I took a big bit out of one of her legs. Oddly my mouth just passed through nothing and when I looked up, I saw this weird big bug horse with a look of horror on her face, protectively holding one of her hooves that happened to have holes in it.
>
> So I was like “Hey mare, don’t you get it?” But she just started flying away on her buggy wings. Some ponies just can’t take a joke, ya know? Anyways um… where was I? I think I kinda lost my train of thought… Well anyways, I guess the whole point I’m trying to make here is, that’s how I got my cutie mark! … Or was that how Equestria was unmade…
>
> In any case, if you get nothing else out of this, I hope I was able to help you understand the true beauty that is Albuckurque Neigh Mexicolt. Oh yeah, and just as a precaution in case you forgot, I do have another little itty bitty bit of advice for any pony or creature reading this. I am hoping you will at least remember this if nothing else. Especially if you ever meet me in pony. I. HATE. SOURGRASS!

“Well that was anticlimactic.” Everypony jumped as the form of Spike seemed to materialize between Pinkie Pie and Twilight.

Pinkie Pie turned to him in surprise. “Spike? When did you get here?”

Twilight answered for her little brother. “Spike’s been here the whole time. I’m surprised you didn’t notice him constantly crawling over us to get comfortable.”

Pinkie just stared ahead blankly. “Huh.”
