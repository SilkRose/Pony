# Brilliant Bakeware

Pinkie jumped up ecstatically. "Oh, can I go first?"

Twilight chuckled. "Sure."

Rainbow Dash rolled her eyes.

Pinkie pulled out another book from the bag, this one had her cutie mark on the cover. "Brilliant Bakeware by Cake Topper. I wonder if their related to the cakes? Wait, this ones about me!"

She excitedly opened the book and began to read…

***

Pinkie was just about to put the cake she was making into the oven when she heard a voice. It was one she'd never heard before, and it sounded like it was right in front of her.

She looked around but couldn't find anypony in sight.

The voice said, "Hey, down here."

Pinkie looked down at the cake.

"Yea, hey, it's me."

Pinkie looked confused. "My cake can talk?"

"No, not the cake, the cake pan."

Before Pinkie could respond, another voice appeared.

"Hey, what are you doing?"

Pinkie replied, "Who, me?"

"No, the cake pan. He's not supposed to be talking."

"Well, why not?"

the cake pan answered, "We're forbidden from talking to ponies, especially bakers."

Pinkie looked sad. "Aww, why not?"

The unknown voice answered, "Bakers are our sworn enemy!"

"But, why? I love all my bakeware…"

The cake pan said, "I know, that is why I chose to speak to you."

The unknown voice revealed itself. "I am your rolling pin. And us bakeware have been sworn to secrecy around ponies. Long ago when our kind was first made, ponies used and abused us."

Pinkie said, "But what about today, everypony I know treats you all well."

The rolling pin continued, "We hold our culture very close, since we cannot move, we can only talk to other bakeware. We know a lot of ponies love us, but tradition dictates we never speak to ponies."

"Well, what happens now that you've spoken to me?"

the cake pan answered, "I spoke to you because I wanted to ask you a favor for all of the bakeware here."

"Oh, what?"

the cake pan almost sounded embarrassed when they asked, "Can you throw us a party? We see glimpses of the parties you throw and we've dreamed about going to one."

Pinkie smiled. "Oh, absolutely!"

At this revelation, all the bakeware started to cheer. Once the cheering settled down they all started introducing themselves to Pinkie.

The cake pan's name was Panny, the rolling pin's name was Pinny. The fridge said they were Refridginald. The toaster was named Diane, after Pinkie of course.

They all had a merry good time as they all finally got a chance to talk with a pony who cared and showed them love.

After everything was said and done, Pinkie went to bed excited to plan the party tomorrow. And the last thing she thought before going to bed was, *I never put the cake in the oven.*

***

Pinkie finished reading the story and smiled. "Aww, that was a nice story."

Fluttershy giggled. "You know what this means?"

Everypony looked at her.

"You're going to have to throw your bakeware a party in real life just in case they are sentient."

Everypony laughed as Pinkie wrote that down in a notepad she pulled from her mane.
