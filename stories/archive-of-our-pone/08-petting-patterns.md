# Petting Patterns

Pinkie looked through the books, trying to find one about her friend. "Here you go!"

Fluttershy took the book from Pinkie and opened it. "Let's see. Performative Petting by Dr. Fauna."

***

Fluttershy sat in her home surrounded by all different kinds of animals. She had just woken up from a nap and was taking in all the noises the animals were making.

Something was wrong, though. And it didn't take her long to figure out what.

The animals weren't making their normal sounds.

Her cats' purr sounded like a bee's buzzing. Her cats' meow sounded like a bear's roar.

Her dogs' bark sounded like hummingbird's chirp.

Every animal sounded different.

Birds sounded like snakes. Snakes sounded like cats. Mice sounded like hyenas. Frogs sounded like fish.

And Angel sounded like Spike.

Fluttershy didn't know what to do. She could still understand what they were saying, but the animals were worried, and so was she.

She tried petting a dog to calm them down, and it barked.

She tried to pet a cat, but it still sounded like a bear. She tried again on the same cat, and they meowed.

She tried more combinations but couldn't figure out why the first pet always worked, but any after failed.

There had to be a system here, but what?

It was at that moment she heard a knock at the door. She answered it and found Spike.

Spike wiggled his ears and sniffed his nose, like Angel would, and said, "Hey Fluttershy, can you help fix my voice, nopony can understand me, or even tell that I'm talking…"

Fluttershy petted Spike and he could talk normally again, but she explained her animal problem and that he would likely revert soon after she pet another animal.

Spike decided to stay and try to help.

The two went back inside and looked at all the animals assembled in her house.

Spike found the materials needed to track their progress and they got to work.

They tried hundreds upon hundreds of different combinations and tactics for how to solve every animals' noises.

The first animal would always succeed, but the second wouldn't. So, there had to be a pattern in there somewhere.

They had been at it for hours when eventually they had found a successful combination of three animals in a row: bear, cat, dog.

They soon realized the pattern was alphabetical. They lined up and sorted all the animals in alphabetical order before going for the last petting parade.

There were a few more failed attempts, but after fixing those errors, they managed to fix every animals sound.

All the animals cheered.

Fluttershy cheered as well. "Yay!"

Spike celebrated. "We did it, Fluttershy!"

They were all happy. The animals went back to doing whatever it was they were doing before the noise mix up happened.

Spike left in good spirits, and Fluttershy sat back down on the couch to relax where she soon fell back asleep.

***

Fluttershy said, "I liked that story."

Pinkie pet Fluttershy. "Yea, it was cute!"

Everypony giggled together.
