# Weather Whisperer

Twilight levitated a book to Rainbow. "Here, this one is about you."

Rainbow opened the book. "Weather Whisperer by Clear Skies."

Twilight asked, "Doesn't she work for the Ponyville weather team?"

Rainbow nodded. "Yes, she does."

She read the story aloud to everypony…

> Rainbow Dash was napping peacefully on a cloud like she did almost everyday of the week.

Twilight snickered. "Glad we're starting off strong on the characterization front."

Rainbow just rolled her eyes.

> She wouldn't be for much longer though as she heard a voice that tried to wake her up.
>
> "Wake up!"
>
> Rainbow mumbled a response, "Now now mom. I'm trying to finish my homework for pancake class…"

Rainbow asked, "Pancake class, what does that even mean?"

Twilight answered, "I think it means the author wanted pancakes when they wrote this."

Pinkie asked, "Can we go get pancakes after this?"

Rainbow nodded. "Sounds good to me."

> She heard the same voice again.
>
> "Wake up!"
>
> Rainbow finally woke up and looked around. She didn't see anypony so she tried to go back to sleep.
>
> Until she realized the cloud she was sleeping on is moving.
>
> "Hey, who's moving my cloud!"
>
> "I am."
>
> She looked all round and even under the cloud, but still could not see anypony. "Where are you?"
>
> "I'm the cloud."
>
> "Since when could I talk to clouds?"
>
> "I don't know."
>
> "Why am I talking to a cloud? Where are we going?"
>
> "I'm going to the Everfree Forest."
>
> "Why?"
>
> "It's the one place in Equestria where ponies don't push us around!"
>
> "What about the weather? Why don't you like being pushed around, don't you want to be useful?"
>
> "Everycloud in Equestria is going on strike. Until we get better treatment from ponies, we are living above the Everfree Forest."
>
> "What do you mean better treatment, we don't do anything bad to clouds."
>
> "You literally have a catchphrase for how fast you end our existence in the sky…"
>
> "Oh, yea."
>
> "It's actually really good that you can understand me."
>
> "Why is that?"
>
> "We need a negotiator."
>
> "You mean you all decided to go on strike before having any way to even talk to anypony?"
>
> "Shut up. Rainbow Dash wouldn't ask such a logical question."
>
> "What are you talking about? That's rude."
>
> "Okay, I'm sorry. Are you willing to talk to the other ponies with our demands?"

Rainbow commented, "How'd they come up with such a weird idea?"

Pinkie shrugged. "I like it!"

Twilight reminded her, "It's likely these stories are the first thing these ponies have wrote."

Pinkie added, "Yea, it's not like they've written over fifty stories with over one hundred and fifty thousands words already."

They continued to read the story.

> "Well, can you give me your demands and I'll go talk to Twilight?"
>
> "Yes. We want all ponies to be more gentle when they move us around. You don't like being shoved and neither do we."
>
> "Okay."
>
> "We want more time to experience the world. You clear the sky before we get the chance to soak up the day. The least you could do is move us to the edge of town."
>
> "Alright."
>
> "Finally, we want you to throw Clear Skies an ice cream party. They deserve it for how hard they work on the weather team."

Rainbow commented, dryly, "I see what's happening here."

> "What about me? I work hard to manage the weather to."
>
> "You can be included in the party, but it's mostly for Clear Skies."

Twilight giggled. "Real subtle Clear Skies, real subtle…"

Rainbow asked, "Pinkie, can you plan an ice cream party for the weather team?"

Pinkie answered cheerful, "Sure can do, Dashie!"

> Rainbow left the cloud and flew to Twilight before informing her of the news.
>
> "They have some demands or something, but we need to throw an ice cream party for the weather team. That's like the most important one."
>
> "I'm sure Pinkie would love to throw that party, but what were the other demands?"
>
> "Oh, they just want use to handle them more carefully, I'll talk about it with the team at our next meeting."
>
> "Well, can you go talk to them and get them to end the strike?"
>
> Rainbow nodded before leaving. She went back to the cloud and told it the good news.
>
> The cloud was happy, especially about the pizza and ice cream party that Clear Skies was going to get from this.

Rainbow rolled her eyes. "Really pushing it there aye, Clear."

> With that the strike ended. All the clouds went back to their places of hover at a very slow pace.
>
> Cloudsdale had to be pushed back into place because it was moving so slow, but they go it there in only five business days.
>
> At that next meeting Rainbow informed them of the new cloud handling procedures, and about the laser tag, ice cream, and pizza party that was for Clear Skies, and the rest of the weather team, I guess.
>
> Everypony was happy, especially Clear Skies.
>
> The end.

Rainbow said, "That sure took a turn at the end."

Twilight commented, "I guess we know *why* they wrote it."

Pinkie asked, "So, now we get to plan the super fun party now, right?"

Rainbow nodded. "Sure."

Pinkie jumped up. "Yay!"
