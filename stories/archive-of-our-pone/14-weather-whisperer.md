# Weather Whisperer

Rainbow opened the book. "Weather Whisperer by Clear Skies."

Twilight asked, "Doesn't she work for the Ponyville weather team?"

Rainbow nodded. "Yes, she does."

She read the story aloud to everypony…

> Rainbow Dash was napping peacefully on a cloud like she did almost everyday of the week.
