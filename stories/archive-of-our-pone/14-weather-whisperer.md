# Weather Whisperer

Twilight levitated a book to Rainbow Dash. "Here. This one's about you."

Rainbow opened it. "Weather Whisperer by Clear Skies."

"Doesn't she work for the Ponyville weather team?" asked Twilight.

Rainbow nodded. She began to read the story aloud.

> Rainbow Dash was napping peacefully on a cloud like she did almost every day of the week.

Twilight snickered. "Glad we're starting off strong on the characterization front."

"Yeah, yeah." Rainbow rolled her eyes. "Do you want me to read this or not?"

> She wouldn't be for much longer, though, as she heard a voice that tried to wake her up.
>
> "Wake up!"
>
> Rainbow mumbled a response, "Not now, Mom. I'm trying to finish my homework for pancake class…"

"Pancake class?" Rainbow scratched her head. "What does that even mean?"

"I think it means the author wanted pancakes when they wrote this," said Twilight. 

Pinkie Pie bounced in place. "Oh! Oh! Can we go get pancakes after this?"

Rainbow nodded. "Sounds good to me."

> She heard the same voice again.
>
> "Wake up!"
>
> Rainbow finally woke up and looked around. She didn't see anypony so she tried to go back to sleep.
>
> Until she realized the cloud she was sleeping on was moving.
>
> "Hey, who's moving my cloud?!"
>
> "I am."
>
> She looked all around and even under the cloud, but still could not see anypony. "Where are you?"
>
> "I am the cloud."
>
> "Since when could I talk to clouds?"
>
> "Don't ask me."
>
> "Why am I talking to a cloud? Where are we going?"
>
> "I'm going to the Everfree Forest because it's the one place in Equestria where ponies don't push us around!"
>
> "What?"
>
> "You heard me."
>
> "But what about the weather? Why don't you like being pushed around, don't you want to be useful?"
>
> "Everycloud in Equestria is going on strike! Until we get better treatment from ponies, we're all gonna live above the Everfree Forest."
>
> "What do you mean better treatment? We don't do anything bad to clouds…"
>
> "You literally have a catchphrase for how fast you end our existence in the sky."
>
> "Oh yeah."
>
> "You know, it's actually really good that you can understand me."
>
> "Why is that?"
>
> "We need a negotiator."
>
> "You mean you all decided to go on strike before having any way to even talk to anypony?"
>
> "Shut up. Rainbow Dash wouldn't ask such a logical question."

"Hey! Rude much?" Rainbow frowned. "And how'd they even come up with such a weird idea?"

Pinkie Pie shrugged. "I like it!"

"It's likely that some of these stories are the first these ponies have ever written," said Twilight.

"Yeah. It's not like they've written over fifty stories with over one hundred and fifty thousands words already!" Pinkie grinned.

Rainbow cleared her throat and continued.

> "Okay, I'm sorry. Are you willing to talk to the other ponies with our demands?"
>
> "Well, can you give me your demands and I'll go talk to Twilight?"
>
> "Yes. We want all ponies to be more gentle when they move us around. You don't like being shoved and neither do we."
>
> "Okay."
>
> "We want more time to experience the world. You clear the sky before we get the chance to soak up the day. The least you could do is move us to the edge of town."
>
> "Alright."
>
> "Finally, we want you to throw Clear Skies an ice cream party. She deserves it for how hard she works on the weather team."

Rainbow sighed. "I can see what's happening here."

> "What about me? I work hard to manage the weather too!"
>
> "You can be included in the party, but it's mostly for Clear Skies."

Twilight giggled. "Real subtle, Clear Skies. Real subtle…"

Rainbow turned. "Hey Pinkie, can you plan an ice cream party for the weather team?"

"Sure can do, Dashie!"

> Rainbow left the cloud and flew to Twilight before informing her of the news.
>
> "They have some demands or something, but we need to throw an ice cream party for the weather team. That's like the most important one."
>
> "I'm sure Pinkie would love to throw that party, but what were the other demands?"
>
> "Oh, they just want us to handle them more carefully, I'll talk about it with the team at our next meeting."
>
> "Well, can you go talk to them and get them to end the strike?"
>
> "Sure," said Rainbow before leaving. She went back to the cloud and told it the good news.
>
> The cloud was happy, especially about the pizza and ice cream party that Clear Skies was going to get from this.

Rainbow rolled her eyes. "Really pushing it there, eh, Clear?"

> With that the strike ended. All the clouds went back to hovering at a very slow pace.
>
> Cloudsdale had to be pushed back into place because it was moving so slow, but, eventually, they got there in only five business days.
>
> At that next meeting, Rainbow informed them of the new cloud handling procedures, and the laser tag, ice cream, and pizza party being held for Clear Skies and the rest of the weather team.
>
> Everypony was happy, especially Clear Skies.
>
> The end.

"Ice cream, pizza, *and* laser tag?" Rainbow shook her head. "Next thing you know, she'll be asking us to crown her Princess!"

"I guess we know *why* she wrote it," said Twilight.

"So, now we get to plan the super fun party, right?" asked Pinkie.

Rainbow nodded. "Sure. Right after we get those pancakes!"

Pinkie grinned. "Yay!"
