# Creature Couture

Twilight said, "Maybe we should find a story that's happier and a little *ahem* let's say tidier! Let's have a look here…"

Twilight sorted through a few of the books before pulling one out, then said "Ah! Here we go! Creature Couture, written by the mayor! This sounds lighthearted and it even comes with a description! Let's see who this story is about then shall we?"

Rarity chuckled. "Well I'd think it would be obvious darling! With the word *couture* in the title it's bound to be about me!"

Twilight shook her head. "This says it's a *carefully crafted critter story* featuring our favourite friend of all fauna, Fluttershy."

Fluttershy gasped. "The mayor… wrote a s-story about me?"

Rainbow Dash urged, "Go ahead! Twi! Read it out!"

***

Today was the day, the bridesmaids were ready, the groom was waiting, the sun was shining… only one major problem. Fluttershy squealed, "Rarity has the feather flu? That means I can't pick up my wedding dress! This is a total catastrophe!"

Angel tapped on Fluttershy's leg as he tried to communicate something, Fluttershy looked down. "What is it Angel? An idea?" Angel gestured to the plethora of animals around, all of them giving sounds of encouragement. Fluttershy's eyes lit up. "Really? You'd all help me make a new wedding dress in time for me to walk down the aisle? That sounds really wonderful!"

The animals got to work, Harry the bear brought leaves and flowers ready to be stitched together, using the thread of a friendly [spider](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dkKt-9QGx-U) who had decided to help, birds fluttered around to put flowers in Fluttershy's mane and a couple of mice were hopping around Fluttershy's legs to curl vines around them. Some butterflies even contributed some pollen to act as a natural perfume! From the tip of her tail to the end of her muzzle, all of them worked to make the perfect wedding dress!

Some of the birds went a step further by adding a loose feather to her leaf dress to add some colour and make it stand out. The spider had done a terrific job of tying together a few twigs into a wonderful makeshift tiara! The twigs had been smoothed out and shaped into a pleasing look by a helpful beaver after a squirrel had been kind enough to fetch them.

Fluttershy looked in the mirror, for something that had been made by woodland animals, it actually looked rather fetching! Fluttershy's eyes welled with tears as she took in her appearance, this would be the most perfect dress for her wedding day! Just in time, she only had a few minutes to get to the ceremony! Fluttershy rushed out, wearing her perfect wedding dress!

***

Fluttershy blushed. "The mayor wrote a story about me getting married? Who am I marrying in the story?"

Twilight shrugged. "It doesn't say, makes sense though, the mayor has officiated a couple of weddings in her time."

Rainbow Dash grimaced. "Yuck! Too mushy! Let's pick something else out…"
