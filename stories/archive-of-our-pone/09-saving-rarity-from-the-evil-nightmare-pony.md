# Saving Rarity from the Evil Nightmare Pony

"This next one will be from, ah- _Sweetie Belle?_" Rarity cleared her throat, rubbing a hoof against the side of her head. "…and it just so happens to be about me and Twilight. How, erm.. kind of her to write about us."

"Ain't that somethin'." Applejack walked over, looking down at the unopened paper. "Better be careful. It could be dangerous."

"Really now, darling." The unicorn raised a brow skeptically. "A story. What's the worst that could happen?"

"Yer askin' that question _now_?"

"That's... fair enough, I suppose." She turned her attention back to the envelope, wincing at the title that appeared as she unfolded the several pages glued together inside. "Well. let's start from the top, shall we?"

***

**"Saving Rarity from the Evil Nightmare Pony"**

"Once upon a time there was a pretty princess called Twilight. She was beautiful and kind and great friends with everypony but she was very very lonely. She was the second best Princess in all of Equestria though so she didn't mind it that much.

_"_**_Second_** _best?"_

_"I'm sure she appreciates the sentiment."_

"One day, she was sent to Ponyville to save everypony there from the return of the Nightmare Moon. She trained for this for ~~days~~ months and months and months, until she was ready to take revenge for Princess Luna!

"But when the Nightmare Moon returned, she trapped ALL of Twilight's friends in the Castle of the Two Sisters! She then said, 'Unless you make me Princess of Equestria, I'll make sure all your friends meet a TERRIBLE END!'

"But Twilight held her ground and said, 'No! You won't get away with this!' and started flying up to the top of the big tower to save her friends from her!

_"She sounds.. distinctly more heroic than I recall," Rarity commented._

_"Ya got that right," the earth pony replied with a sage nod. "Feel like the story's missin' a few panic attacks here and there. Other than that, pretty alrigh' retelling so far."_

"When Nightmare Moon saw it though she started laughing evilly and DESTROYED THE TOWER, flying away and leaving them to fall to their deaths!

"All of her friends started falling except Rainbow Dash because she was cool and could fly. And also Fluttershy.

"But Rainbow Dash and Fluttershy could only save two of their friends! So Rarity kept on falling and falling until suddenly- WHAM! Twilight swept in and saved her in the nick of time, catching her mid-fall.

"'Oh, Twilight, you saved me!' Rarity cried out. 'I owe you my life! How can I ever repay you?' And then Twilight said 'It's okay Rarity , we're friends and that's all that matters.' But Rarity's heart was beating faster than ever now and she fainted right there!

_"Huh. Well."_

_"'Well'? Well indeed! Is Sweetie Belle implying I have a crush on Twilight? I do not! That is absolutely ridiculous!"_

_Applejack shrugged. "You could convince me otherwise, just for the record." Rarity put down the letter, picked up its envelope and smacked the earth pony on the muzzle. "Ay- buck was that for?"_

_"I do not have a crush on- on Twilight Sparkle of all ponies! I'm simply offended that this was the best Sweetie could come up with! Sweeping me away from falling to my death? She didn't even have wings then!"_

_"Seems like you've worked yourself up over her and she ain't even here for today's readin'- ay!" She was met with another light whack against the face as the envelope fell to the floor. "Alright, alright, I get it! Let's finish this up."_

_The unicorn nodded, an intense venom coloring her tone. "Sounds wonderful, sweetheart."_

"When she woke up Twilight was already by her bed. She said 'I'm glad you're okay rarity' and hugged her and Rarity hugged her back. And also Rainbow Dash was ~~their~~ there because she's super cool and she said 'I knew she would be fine cause Rarity's awesome'!

"And then everypony had a group hug and Luna and Celestia was there too and thanked Twilight for saving her. The end."

***

"I think I'm done for today, actually."

"Really? This is only the fifth one we've gone through tonight."

Rarity took the story and folded it up, resealing the envelope. "I need to have a chat with Sweetie Belle. I'll be back... in a few hours, alright?" She smiled pleasantly and walked out the front door as Twilight came from a different room.

She stared at the door, the mare leaving through it, then at Applejack. "Did I... miss something?"

"Yeah. The story that sealed Sweetie Belle's fate, I would think." Applejack shrugged and turned her attention back to the stack of mail. "Not much else."

"Oh, alri- _WHAT?"_
