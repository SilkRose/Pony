# Eyes

Spike glanced up from his comic book to see a pair of eyes rolling down the floor of the library. He didn't think much of it and went back to his graphic novel.

"Spike?" Twilight asked from down the hall, "have you seen a pair of eyes?" she finished before loudly bumping into a wall.

"Yeah," Spike said, "they went that way."

"Well I don't know where that way is, Spike."

"Why not?" Spike turned the page. "I just pointed that way."

"Because," Twilight said, finally in the room. Spike looked up and gasped.

"Where are your eyes, Twilight?!"

Twilight motioned in the direction she thought her eyes rolled down but was wrong. "I accidentally zapped my eyes out of my head attempting to cast a periscopic sight spell." Twilight fumbled around, bumping into random things. "I suppose the spell would still be a success if they didn't just pop out and get the better of me."

Spike was visibly disconcerted. "C-could you put on a pair of sunglasses at least so you look cool instead of weird?"

Twilight's horn glowed, and a pair of sunglasses appeared on the back of her head. "Spike?"

Spike got up, shivered, and corrected the sunglasses so they covered her eyeholes. "T-that's better."

"Okay," Twilight said, "time to find my eyes!"

Spike gnarred in agreement and stumbled in the direction he saw the eyes roll. Unfortunately the door to the new library was open so Twilight's eyes could be anywhere. "You stay here and do math in your head, Twi. I'm gonna find your eyes."

"I think I could still help--"

"How? You're as blind as a bat."

Twilight cradled her chin for a second before it hit her. "Like a bat! I could use echolocation to approximate the surrounding area and provide a mental image of the area!"

"O-okay, if you insist," Spike said, "just take my claw as an extra measure."

Twilight held out her hoof and Spike grabbed it with his claw, leading her out of the library.

"Has anyone seen a pair of eyes?" Spike yelled into Ponyville. Everyone stared at him like he was asking where a set of eyes rolled off to.

"Twilight, I don't think anyone's seen your eyes–" Spike started before he lay his eyes on Angel Bunny, who had the eyes.

"Twilight, we're good, Angel has your eyes." Spike walked towards Angel, who guarded the eyes from Spike. Spike frowned.

"I think he's trying to negotiate the release of your eyes, Twilight."

"Hmm." Twilight thought for a second before her horn glowed. Nothing happened until every pet in Ponyville started yapping and yowling. Angel covered his ears and winced.

"We can do this the easy way or the hard way, Angel," Twilight said.

"This is ridiculous, I wouldn't be that mean to Angel," Twilight said as she read over the story.

Fluttershy raised a hoof. "You did kick him into a mud puddle once."

Twilight made a sound of objection and disbelief. "That was different! My leg just slipped."

"And I had to clean Angel up afterwards," Fluttershy said.

"Sometimes hooves get twitchy. That's not my fault."
