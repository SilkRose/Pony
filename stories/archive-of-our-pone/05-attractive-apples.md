# Attractive Apples

Applejack pulled a book from the pile. "Let's see here. This ones about apples. Attractive Apples by Doctor Whooves."

***

If you're reading this, it means I'm gone… It's hard to explain, but I'm not a normal pony…

Wait, wrong book.

Ahem.

It was a normal day at Sweet Apple Acres. Normal as it could be inside of this story. Harvest season was afoot. Do ponies say ahoof instead? It doesn't matter.

Applejack had just bucked a tree. She watched as the apples fell. They weren't falling like she expected. No, these apples were falling like something was different about them, yes.

As they landed she noticed they weren't obeying the laws of physics, or at least what she knew of them. Sometimes I like to break those laws, but only when nopony is looking.

The apples clumped together on the ground, like they were attracted to each other. She didn't understand so she pushed on the clumps of apples.

They rolled on the ground and stuck together as one singular appile.

She pressed harder on the next push and managed squish one of the apples, but this didn't change much. The squished apple just attracted to the other apples filling in the gaps.

She was at a loss for words and apples. So, she decided to bring the apples to somepony who might know.

On her way to said yet-to-be-named pony, she was stopped by various ponies in town. Each with their own troubles.

The first pony said, "Applejack, my son ate an apple and now the tinfoil is stuck to his coat!"

The second said, "I need your help, I keep stealing ponies' jewelry when I walk by. It started happening after I ate an apple, but I don't know why that would cause this."

The third pony didn't talk, he just opened his mouth and made harmonica noises.

Applejack ignored them. She had science to do.

Oh, that pony who doesn't have a name yet? Their name is Twilight.

Twilight opened her door as soon as Applejack arrived.

She showed her the apples and their behavior, and mentioned the ponies asking for help.

Twilight concluded that her apples were magnetic when she held them up to a magnet and they stuck together.

But she wondered why would they be magnetic?

Twilight did a quick three-month geological survey of the ground at Sweet Apple Acres.

The results, as speedy as they were, were conclusive.

There was too much iron in the soil. That's definitely how magnets work, no need to look it up.

You might be wondering, how do they fix this problem?

Obviously, the answer is a bigger magnet. Twilight used her levitation to scan a big magnet over all the trees in the fields. It successfully pulled out the bits of iron.

What did they do with the iron? Why do you have so many questions?

They refined it and turned it into scientific equipment, the best kind of equipment.

The end.

Applejack seemed dissatisfied. "That was a mess."

Twilight chuckled. "A lot of these ponies are probably writing their first story."

Applejack nodded.
