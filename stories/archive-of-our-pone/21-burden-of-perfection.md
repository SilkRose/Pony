# Burden of Perfection

Rainbow Dash noticed a story written on slices of tree bark and bound by vines fashioned into rope. She lifted it and one of the rings burst into dust. "Hey, guys? Maybe we should do this one next before it's gone?" The cover had only a title carved in: _Burden of Perfection_. No author or identifying marks existed on its exterior, nor its opening pages.

***

Once upon a time, Rainbow Dash was born.

She was perfect from her first second alive.

This was an undeniable truth.

She was raised by the adoration of those around her. Not a moment passed where she was not reminded of her superiority.

She was the best, and she was acutely aware there existed no other to contest her for that title.

Yet, for all the adoration she had, she always craved more.

Needed more.

Outside of her home existed countless ponies who did not give her their attention so readily, and she needed it to be satiated.

It was cruel, was it not? That she was forced by nature to need the hearts of anyone but those who already gave theirs willingly?

The other ponies were not pegasi. They did not understand why her flying was so impressive. They could never feel in their hearts why flying itself mattered.

Only pegasi could be so uniquely privileged.

Where they should have seen regality, they only saw something that was not them. They had to live with their mediocrity instead of her glory.

Their inferiority.

Their inability to understand that she was the one who deserved their worship.

Yet, a challenger arose: Twilight Sparkle. She was wholly unaware of her destined rival, yet she went to work stealing everything that was already rightfully claimed.

She gained all the other ponies' admiration overnight.

Rainbow Dash, for all her efforts, was left with nothing to show for it.

Then,

Then, Twilight Sparkle did the most insulting, humiliating, reviling thing she could have done.

She offered a hoof.

She found Rainbow Dash and held out a hoof.

"We needn't be enemies", she mocked.

"We can work together. We can be allies. We can even be friends."

Her voice was poisoned honey. She knew all she said was tempting. She knew the temptation would be great.

Yet, Rainbow refused.

She refused to settle.

She refused to admit defeat or accept a consolation prize.

She was better than Twilight Sparkle in every way. Bargaining was beneath her.

Rainbow Dash returned to her pegasi and they moved their clouds higher and higher until the ponies below could never even know of them anymore.

Time passed and they remained isolated. It was for the best. Clearly, they were unwanted. Clearly her greatness was wasted on those below.

At times, they would fly down.

They could not live off clouds and sunlight.

There was never enough for them, but it only made them stronger.

They hardly complained.

They still had their shining jewel.

They still had their Rainbow Dash to worship.

Rainbow Dash would still fly down herself at times. Sometimes, to remind them of her greatness. Sometimes to show off her flying and take their attention.

Even once to crash Pinkie Pie's wedding.

She hardly even put a dent in the festivities.

Hardly anything to hold such a grudge over.

Then, she came up with her most glorious plan.

She tricked all the most important ponies below to gather for her.

When they were all together, she flew down as fast as she could to perform her greatest move: the sonic rainbow.

Everyone would have no choice but to love her.

Yet, she did not anticipate what would happen when her pegasi saw the ground again.

They grew weak. They yearned for more of Twilight Sparkle's land below.

They traded Rainbow Dash for her.

And then Rainbow Dash was alone.

Twilight Sparkle did not extend a hoof again.

Neither did Pinkie Pie or Applejack or even Rarity.

It was Spike who did.

Such an insignificant creature in all thing.

Of course Rainbow Dash could never accept it.

She could never except such a downgrade.

An insult.

And so she flew.

She was too perfect for them all.

Even if she had to be perfect alone.

She would remain perfect.

She would remain flying.

She would not be humiliated by those far beneath her.

Tell me.

Does this story still seem fair when it's her?

***

"I, um, Yeah, I don't think I liked that one. I'd never do any of that. Loyalty is my whole thing, and everypony always loves me, so…"

Twilight carefully took the book from Rainbow Dash. "I don't think it was really supposed to be about any of us." She set it somewhere it would not be damaged any further.

Rarity looked around the room. "So, everypony here is in agreement that was a completely delusional account of things?"

Twilight stared at the book for a few moments. "I should take a trip somewhere after we're done here."

Pinkie bobbed her head as she said in sing-song, "At least I got married~"

The room laughed, and tried to move on.
