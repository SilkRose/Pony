Pinkie brought a book to Rarity. "Look, we got a book from Coriander! You want to read it?"

Rarity took the book in her magic an opened it up. "Sure. I hope they're still doing well."

A small card fell out from the book. Rarity picks it up and read it aloud.

"Saffron and I are still doing well, thanks to you! I decided to write something for your library to try and repay everything you have done for us. Saffron is more than embarrassed with my story however, so I believe I have done well."

Pinkie commented, "Sounds like we have something interested to read!"

> Delicious Dresses
>
> By Coriander Cumin
>
> Rarity was in the middle of designing her latest creation. She had been on a roll, with new customers coming to buy dresses daily at this point. Everypony in town had to own at least one of her dresses at this point, but weirdly, she didn't see many mares out and about in them.
>
> She didn't mind however, as long as the customer was happy, she was happy.

Rarity said, "This is getting off to a good start."

Pinkie nodded.

> She was about to put the finishing touches onto the current work in progress when she heard a knock at the door.
>
> "Coming," she said in her usual elegant voice as she set her utensils down and headed for the door. Once at the door she opened it and found her dear friend Pinkie Pie, whom she greeted. "Hello, darling."
>
> "Hi, Rarity. Do you want to go with me to the new dresstaurant in town?"

> Rarity was confused, she had to have heard her friend wrong. "Dresstaurant?"

> "Yes, its this new style of restaurant, I've never been, but it sounded interesting!"
>
> "Well, I guess I can go. I needed a break from all the orders lately anyway."

> "Yippee!" Pinkie said before they made their way to the new establishment.

Rarity commented, dryly, "I have a feeling were getting to the embarrassing part."

Pinkie giggled.

> They trotted through the front door where they met their hostess.

> The prices on the dresses were increased over what she charged to make them. I guess they had to make a profit, but how could ponies casually afford the price of a nice fancy dress like it was a meal at a restaurant. Rarity didn't understand it.
>
> She would soon though, as a new pony appeared at their table.
>
> "Hello, my name is Edible Pants, but most ponies call me Eddie Bull. I'm the head chef here. I wanted to personally serve you two. It's not everyday our main supplier eats at our dresstaurant."

Rarity asked with a giggle, "Wonder if he's related to Fancy Pants."

> He was Fancy Pants' younger, less successful, less handsome, less fancy, more pants-ed brother.

Pinkie protested in agreement. "Hey, yeah! I don't think I've ever seen him wear pants."

Both mares giggled together.

> Edible continued, "Satisfaction guaranteed, or I'll eat my hat!"
