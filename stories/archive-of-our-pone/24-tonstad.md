# Tonstad

Ponyville Ikea opened to great fanfare. The colossal blue-and-yellow block towered over the small town, with seven Swedish flags proudly flapping in the wind. Ponies filtered into the rotating door out the front, in awe of the mere existence of an Ikea in their area. Manehattan wept like a widow, Fillydelphia raged at the Heavens, Canterlot pretended it didn’t care, but they could do nothing but accept that the store was planted firmly at the East side of Ponyville.

“Well, girls, this is it. If this is your first time in Ikea, prepare to be amazed,” Twilight announced. The other day she had attempted to write a letter to the council about the five inch deep pothole outside her castle, but, to her horror, her writing desk had vanished. She’d bet her bottom bit it was Spoiled Rich that stole it. She’d been complaining about a bad back and the surprising danger of shattered glass for a few days, but Twilight needed more solid evidence to make a conviction.

“Twailight, why are ya’ll starin’ inta space like that? Howdy, howdy,” Applejack drawled Southernly.

“Sorry, I was thinking about something else. As I was saying, this Ikea is what will really put Ponyville on the map.”

“Can we go inside now?” Rainbow asked impatiently.

“I’d like to try the meatballs,” Fluttershy whispered. “I love animals. I wonder how they taste.”

“Not yet, Flutters, we need to wait for Rarity. This is a mane six type of adventure, so we all have to all be here,” Twilight stated.

As if on cue, a sudden flash of magic deposited a letter into Twilight’s hooves. She read it, and her blood ran so cold her veins grew icicles. Dear Twilight, I will not be complicit in the erasure and erosion of Equestrian culture by Swedish imperialism. Swedish stores belong on Swedish soil. Rarity - P.S. you still need to return my mane brush. It’s been a week.

“Twilight, I think Rarity might have some uncool vibes,” Rainbow pointed out, having overheard Twilight’s silent reading of the letter.

“I’ll talk to her about that later. But for now, let’s finally immerse ourselves in the perfection of Ikea.” Twilight led the way, avoiding the rotating door because some colt had somehow got his entire body wedged in it. Like, it was pushing against his ribs and stuff, the security guards were staring dumbfounded at it. Genuinely stressful sight to behold.

“I’m here too!” Pinkie Pie yelled, suddenly realizing she’d been quiet for too long.

The inside of the store was lined with the most amazing flatpack furniture the world had ever had the pleasure of containing. The Germans had their beer, the Belgians their chocolate, the Colombians something I can’t bring up in an E-rated fic, but the Swedes had their furniture.

“Wow!” Twilight said, trotting over to the desks. They looked immaculate. Perfectly sanded wood, ergonomic in all the right places, a humanist and economic design… Twilight could feel her Cutie Mark almost warping itself into a Lagkapten / Alex.

…

Meanwhile, Rainbow Dash saw something that caught her magenta eyes. The racecar bed. A bedroom staple for all children who will grow up to be philosophers and world leaders. “This is radical!” she yelled, “I don’t know what it's supposed to be because I’ve never seen a race car before, but I bet it can go fast. Like me.” She turned to read the information plaque. It was only ninety bits. It was for foals, but, if Rainbow curled into a ball and held her knees to her chest, she could maybe squeeze in.

She thought for a few seconds.

“No.” She turned away, dejected, “I can’t. If my GP heard I was sleeping in a position like that, he’d have a heart attack…”

…

Meanwhile, Applejack had accosted a member of staff, and was ready to fire off the questions in her (metaphorical) rootin’ tootin’ Ol’ Western six-shooter that shoots questions instead of bullets. “Howdy-doo, pardner? Does that there Sweden land have them there heehaw cowboys?”

“No.” The store assistant stared for a second, then remembered he was there to sell stuff. “Would you like to buy a pencil?”

“Does that there Sweden whatchimajig have them there spittons? Howdy howdy?”

“What? Madam, I… what are you asking?”

“Does it have those ol’ Western yeehaw’ pig squealin’ steam locomotive contraptions? Howdy.”

The staff member’s expression dropped. He picked up his walkie-talkie. “Uh, I think we have a customer- She’s saying- She’s not making any sense. I think she might need some medical attention. Can we get somepony down here to- Yeah, yeah, like, it’s nonsensical words. Just send somepony down here…”

…

Meanwhile, Fluttershy ate five meatballs. Meat. M. E. A. T. She knew what they were as well. She wasn’t tricked or ignorant or anything, she knew what she was eating the entire time.

She liked them.

…

Meanwhile, Pinkie Pie bounced around the store and stuff. “DJUNGLORM!” she giggled, looking at the weird and funny word used to refer to the snake plushies. “Hahaha! BLÅHAJ HAAAHAHA!” She continued bouncing and laughing at the strange-looking words. “BILLY!” She laughed so hard she nearly threw up. “That’s just a GUY’S NAME! Swedish is my FAVOURITE SILLY LANGUAGE!”

“PINKIE!” Twilight snapped out of her furniture-induced trance. “Come here, come over here with me.”

“What? What did I…”

“Just… Just come over here. You’re not in trouble, I just need to explain this okay?”

Pinkie obliged and huddled into the corner with Twilight. She turned to face her purple friend.

“Look, Pinkie,” Twilight said grimly, “I know it looks funny, but these are real words. It’s okay if you think they look odd, but announcing that you think Swedish is ‘silly’ could come off as, well… a little racist.”

“But, I’m not racist! I love races!”

“I know, I know Pinkie. But you might make others think you are. I’ll sweep this under the rug, but, please, in the future, be more tactful around other cultures, okay?”

“GIRLS! OH MY ACTUAL GOSH!” Rainbow yelled, suddenly appearing behind the pair. “Applejack got taken to A&E ‘cos she was acting odd. I’ll fly over there and see what’s goi-”

Fluttershy meekly approached the others, her snout covered in that ambrosia-for-mortals known as Ikea gravy, her stomach 5 times larger than medically possible. “I want to eat more animals.”

5 hours later…

“Turns out, I had some Poison Joke lodged in ma nose that was makin’ me act like a slightly offensive stereotype,” Applejack explained.

The ponies stood around the newly assembled Tonstad. Rainbow Dash insisted she make it alone because she just… She just doesn’t want others breathing down her neck. Okay? You need space to make these things, and too many hooves will get in the way and cause problems… Okay? So the others watched Fluttershy inhale a forty ounce steak like some freakshow at a classic P.T. Barnum circus thingy.

Anyway, Rarity looked at the table as if it just insulted her lineage. “This isn’t Equestrian made.”

“I know. Rarity, I always thought you were more cosmopolitan? I know it’s different, but…”

“It’s Satanic.”

“What does this table have to do with Satan? Look, Rarity, just… Give it a chance, okay?”

“Twilight said hating Swedes is racist, Rarity! Discriminating others for the circumstances of their birth is immoral, as it increases social tensions, rejects the beneficial and interesting contributions of other cultures, and results in judging others for traits out of their control,” Pinkie Pie lectured.

“I see the Devil smiling at me when I look into it,” Rarity replied, glaring at the apparently devil-worshiping table.

“I actually know a Satanist,” Rainbow Dash yelled, “and he’s chill. He introduced me to Mareilyn Manson.”

As Fluttershy mumbled something about her preference for Skinny Puppy’s discography, then silently complained about a stomach ache, Applejack turned, “Twailight, actions speak louder than words. Give that thingimajig the stress test.”

“Right.” She picked up a heavy stack of books that did NOT contain Fifty Shades of Grey (and if it did, it was a gift. And Twilight wouldn’t have read it.) and carried it over to the nascent Tonstad. The twelve-book-high pile loomed over the table, and then, with the fury of a scorned goddess, she slammed it onto the table. “See?” she said smugly, “quality building standards! If it can stand that, it can-”

The table collapsed. Completely gave way. Like, utterly dismantled in the blink of an eye.

“Horrid,” Rarity judged.

“No! No, I swear-”

“Why’d you slam it down so hard, Twi?” Rainbow asked.

“To prove its strength! I mean, what if I had a stack of books and a candle, and the candle went onto the books and then the fire and the castle became in flames and I was inside so I was burned to death?”

“Well said, Twilight!” Pinkie exclaimed while juggling cupcakes for some godforsaken reason.

“My tummy hurts,” Fluttershy moaned before curling into the background.

Rarity made a deep inhale, probably sucking in a lungful of air that her vocal chords would manipulate into the single most racist thing uttered in Equestria, but Applejack turned. “Ah told ya’ll we should’a nailed and glued and screwed it together. That’s how ah do it on tha farm. Why, Apple Bloom’s rockin’ horse probably has more nails in it than a nail tornado in a nail factory.”

“No, no, Applejack, you don’t need those for Ikea products. It has dowels that keep everything locked-”

“Dowels? What are those?” Rainbow asked.

“You should know, you assembled it!”

“Yeah? I just-”

Twilight looked deeply into Rainbow Dash’s moderate cerise eyes. “Did you read the instructions?”

“No?! Reading the instructions is for women!”

“YOU ARE A WOMAN!”

The room went silent, aside for the soft pitter-patter of Pinkie’s juggled cupcakes.

After several seconds of cavernous silence, Rarity piped up. “Well, this is getting heated. I bet the Säpo are behind this. Somehow.”

Rainbow Dash’s #a8026f eyes looked at Rarity. “Rares, enough with that. Look, let’s just chill, okay? I should’ve read the table recipe, and that’s my error. We’ll make Twi’s desk, get over our sudden prejudices, and be the friends we always were.”

A genuine coolness drifted over the room. The six friends huddled together, smiling and laughing. The day was stranger than most, but that won’t stop their friendship. Twilight’s desk would be a great piece of furniture, but it would also be a monument to their combined friendship!

Then Fluttershy threw up.

Twilight set the book down. She paused for a second, probably weighing up the social impact of an Ikea manifesting in Ponyville.

“Twilight,” Rarity perked up, turning to Twilight, “I have no idea… I love the Swedish! I love Sweden! I would never…” she froze suddenly, unable to compute how out of character she was.

“I know, Rarity…” Twilight stared for a few seconds, “I know.”
