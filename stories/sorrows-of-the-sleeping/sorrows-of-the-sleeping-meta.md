# Sorrows of the Sleeping

## Synopsis:
Fluttershy is having a bad dream about getting rejected by Applejack and grabs her hand in her sleep. This wakes Applejack up who tries to calm her down while also having a crush on her.

## Description:
Fluttershy and Applejack try to calm each other's sorrows while dealing with their own feelings.
