# Sorrows of the Sleeping

Fluttershy squirmed in her makeshift bed on the floor of Pinkie's bedroom. A nightmare playing out inside her head.

She muttered in her sleep, "No… Appleja… I didn't."

Twitching again, her arm shot out from under the blanket, landing on top of Applejack's arm.

Applejack woke up from the sudden touch, and turned to look at Fluttershy.

"Fluttershy, you awake?" Applejack asked all gentle like.

Fluttershy jolted again. "Applejack…"

"Fluttershy?" Applejack tried again.

She only received more muttering.

"I didn't… Sorry…"

She was about to try and wake her up when Fluttershy moved her arm again, this time it landed with Fluttershy's hand on top of Applejack's own.

Applejack blushed at the realization, but before she could pull her hand away, Fluttershy grabbed it in her sleep and squeezed tight.

She resolved to wake her up, she was clearly having a nightmare and wanted her friend.

Applejack gently shook her friend. "Fluttershy, wake up."

Her blush had intensified at this point, but she didn't care. Her friend needed her and not even a little embarrassment would stop her from helping.

"Fluttershy, wake up!" She tried to whisper loudly.

Finally it worked as her eyes shot open and she froze up.

"Fluttershy, are you okay? You sounded like you was having a nightmare or something."

When she got no reply, Applejack squeezed her hand a little tighter. "Listen here Fluttershy, you can tell me anything, I'm here for you. If you be honest with me, I'll be honest with you."

Fluttershy closed her eyes as a blush formed on her face. In the tiniest voice Applejack had ever heard, Fluttershy said, "I like you."

Applejack relaxed a bit. "Well, to tell you the truth, sugarcube, I like you too."

Fluttershy squeaked out, "You do?"

"I do. Why do you think I slept next to you at this here sleepover. It's because I want to be closer with you."

"I'm glad you did…"

"Me too. I'm not good at this romance and feeling stuff, but I know I felt different around you."

"I do too."

"I didn't say anything because I was afraid you would be too shy to say anything. I didn't realize you had feelings too."

"I was too shy to express them. I'm sorry…"

"Don't be sorry, you got them out one way or another."

"Applejack?"

"Yes, sugarcube?"

"Thank you, for waking me up from my nightmare."

"Don't mention it."

"Uhm, Applejack?"

"Yeah?"

"I think you're really pretty."

Applejack's blush intensified. "I think you're pretty darn pretty, too."

Fluttershy blushed more as they laid in silence.
