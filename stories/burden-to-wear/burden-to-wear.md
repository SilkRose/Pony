# Burden to Wear

Rarity slipped on a dress she found while on their latest group adventure saving the world. Her friends had said to leave it, but she just had to have it. Twas a blue thing with gold accents, it matched her perfectly.

As the dress settled around her form she felt an energy shift around her. She felt beautiful, she felt pretty, and she felt confident. She was going to show her friends it was a good idea, and she was going to do it in style.

She found Twilight reading a book in public, nothing unusual for her. What was different was this book was about dresses. Rarity sure wouldn't have expected Twilight to be into dresses.

"Twilight, I never know you liked to read about dresses!" Rarity smiled at her friend.

Twilight looked confused. "I'm not reading about dresses, I'm reading about geothermal currents."

Rarity shakes her heard, brushing it off. "What do you think of this fabulous dress I found? You all said to leave it, but I do look amazing in it, do I not?"

Twilight shrugged, "It looks nice."

The dress tightens around Rarity, but she doesn't notice, too busy thinking about Twilight's slight of dress. "Uhh, this dress is beautiful, however could you say it is just 'nice'."

Rarity storms off to find another friend.

***

She finds Pinkie giving out free balloons in front of Sugarcube Corner.

"Hello Pinkie, do you like this beautiful blue dress I'm wearing?"

Pinkie looks confused. "That dress isn't blue, it's pink."

Rarity looks down at the dress, and sure enough, it's pink. "Ah, just one of the many amazing qualities of my dress, it changes colors!"

"I bet you could make a dress that does that, instead of just finding it."

"I never told you that was this dress."
