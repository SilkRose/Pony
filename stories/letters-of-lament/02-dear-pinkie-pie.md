# Dear Pinkie Pie

Dear Pinkie Pie,

You have nothing to apologize for, you did nothing wrong. I think this is just one big misunderstanding. I'm sorry that you ever felt we weren't friends. I love you and you're my bestest friend!

I should be the one apologizing, I was the one who raised my voice, but I never expected you to react like this. I'm sorry I didn't communicate my feelings in a more constructive manner. I wasn't mad at you for having feelings for me. I was trying to get your attention.

Pinkie Pie, I like you, like a lot a lot. Like romantically. I want to date you. I wanted to make it official and ask you out. You weren't responded to me, then when my plan was going perfectly, I got frustrated and raised my voice.

I assume you were lost in thought while I was trying to be romantic and ask you out. When you didn't reply, I tried to snap you out of it, but eventually you looked sad and I gave up.

So, I went to my bed and cried, thinking I had lost my chance to be with you. I don't know what you heard when I was trying to get your attention, but what I heard from your silence made me feel the worst I've ever felt.

Looks like it's my time to apologize to you.

I'm sorry that I didn't hold that hug even longer and tell you that I loved it.

I'm sorry I didn't tell you how much I loved you holding my hoof.

I'm sorry for not asking you out when you were laying on my chest.

I'm sorry I failed to write you a poem expressing my feelings for you after yours.

I'm sorry I didn't give you one of your heart shaped pancakes and tell you it was my heart.

I'm sorry you ever slept in a different bed from me that night.

I'm sorry I didn't tell you how much you mean to me when you woke up in my hooves.

I'm sorry I didn't immediately kiss you back.

I'm sorry that you had to feel this pain for that long because of me.

I'm sorry you ever had to write that letter.

With all that said, I have one thing left to ask.

Pinkie Pie, will you go out with me?

Love,  
Twilight Sparkle

P.S. – You're super duper pretty too!
