# Bedtime Burdens

Twilight was just about to head off to bed when she heard a knock on the library's front door.

*Who could that be?*

She made her way to the door and answered it, finding Pinkie Pie.

"Hi Twilight. I was wondering if I could sleep over at your place for a few days?"

Twilight looked puzzled. "Sure, but why do you need to stay with me?"

"I loaned my bed to the traveling circus in town. They needed a bed for some seriously danger stunts."

Twilight accepted the answer. "Sure, I have a spare bed in my loft."

Pinkie looks at Twilight sheepishly. "I also loaned them your spare bed…"

Twilight sighed. "Do we at least get free tickets for loaning out our property?"

Pinkie nodded enthusiastically. "We can go see our beds live!"

Twilight didn't exactly understand, but she didn't mind.

Until she realized what that meant.

*I'm going to be sharing my bed with Pinkie.*

She tried to play it cool. "Uh, why don't you come in and we can… sleep?"

*Great job…*

Pinkie smiled. "I love sleep."

Twilight leads them to her bed where they lay down back to back.

Pinkie says, "Goodnight, Twilight."

Twilight replies, "Goodnight, Pinkie."

Twilight laid awake unable to sleep. Her mind racing around the pony pressed to her back.

*I can't believe were sharing a bed.*

*I just wish I had the courage to tell her about my feelings.*

*I still don't understand why they needed beds, but if it helps me confess to Pinkie, I won't complain.*

*How is it going to help…*

*I guess it means spending more time with Pinkie.*

A smile came to her lips as she thought of her.

Twilight rolled over and rests her head on Pinkie's mane.

She whispered to her, "I love you, Pinkie."

She finally calmed herself down and managed to fall asleep.

***

The next day went by in a blur to Twilight. She spent the whole day having fun with Pinkie. they even went to the circus and saw their own beds performing in the show.

Bedtime came quicker than Twilight was ready for.

They laid down back to back like the night before.

Twilight asked, "Pinkie?"

"Yes?"

Twilight's voice rose in pitch. "Is it okay if I face you instead of sleeping like last night?"

"Sure, anything is fine with me if it makes your more comfortable."

Twilight smiled and rolled over to face Pinkie. "I had a lot of fun with you today."

Pinkie said, "I had a lot of fun too!" Her voice raised like Twilight's. "Can I face you too?"

Twilight, unsure of how to hide the blush she just realized she had, replied, "Sure, but is it okay if we keep our eyes closed?"

"Absolutely."

With that they both closed their eyes and Pinkie rolled to face Twilight.

Twilight giggled as she felt Pinkie poking her to find where she was. "I'm sorry for the weird request…"

Pinkie smiled. "Oh, it's fine. It's just like a new little game, right?"

Twilight smiled. "Right."



***

Twilight couldn't sleep. She had laid there for over an hour with her back pressed against Pinkie Pie's. Who had been asleep almost the whole time. Twilight wanted to cry.

All she had to do was tell Pinkie how she felt, but here she lay.

Twilight finally broke down and cried into her hooves. She tried to cry quietly, hoping Pinkie wouldn't wake up.

*Why is this so hard?*

*Why can't I just tell her how I feel?*

*We're literally sharing a bed.*

As her mind emptied of thoughts, she continued to cry. A cry of defeat, and of loneliness.

But, after a time, somepony took notice.

"Twilight?"

Getting no response, she tried again. "Twilight, are you crying?"

Twilight refused to answer, she didn't know what to say or how to explain this.

Pinkie rotated in the bed before she wrapped her hooves around Twilight.

"Twilight, I love you, and I'll always be here for you."

Pinkie held her tight and nuzzled the back of her mane.

They stayed like this for a time, but as Twilight cried her last tear, she decided to face Pinkie.

Twilight rolled in the bad and faced Pinkie. Their eyes met for a brief time before Twilight buried her head into Pinkie's chest.

Pinkie asked, "Is there anything I can do to make you feel better?"

Twilight muttered, "Hold me…"

Pinkie does as requested and held Twilight tight.

The two mares stayed like this the whole night, as they both fell asleep soon after.
