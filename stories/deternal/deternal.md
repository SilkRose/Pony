# Deternal

Twilight Sparkle stood in front of her bed as her lifelong friends slept around her. Her horn lit up as her magic reached out, engulfing each of her friends' hearts in her magic. Lastly, she wrapped her own heart in the same aura.

For a moment, she just stood there, feeling the rhythmic beating of each heart. As time escaped her, she felt as the influence of her magic tuned all six hearts into beating synchronously. She took a deep breath and began to recite the incantations of the spell.

"Lifespans limited by time, link my friends onto mine. A curse of weakness singed on my heart, for upon each death I will hurt."

She felt a weakness in her legs as the realization of what she'd just done hits her. She falls to the floor as tears fall from her eyes. She was never going to outlive her friends, she just had to convince herself it was the right decision.

As she lies there crying in a pile of herself, one of her friends takes notice. Pinkie Pie sees her friend in despair and goes over to comfort her. She wraps her hooves around her. "Twilight, are you okay?"

Twilight wraps her hooves around Pinkie. "Pinkie…"

"Yes?"

Twilight sniffled. "I need you to promise me…"

"What? Anything for you!"

Twilight hugs Pinkie tighter. "Promise me you'll never leave me…"

Pinkie did the motions and the saying before adding, "I would never leave you, Twilight. You're one of my best friends."

"I love you girls so much. I never want to lose any of you."

"We'll always be here for you."

"I sure hope so."

Pinkie tightened her hold on Twilight as Twilight rested her head on Pinkie.

Twilight was almost a head taller than her friends at this point. She saw her own growth as a threat to their friendships. What was she going to do if her friends died and she lived forever. Well, now she wouldn't. Now, she would never have to leave her friends again.

Pinkie asked, "What's got you all sad? I know I'll never leave you… did something happen to make you think I would?"

Twilight faltered. She didn't know what to say. It was too late for her friends to convince her not to cast the spell, and telling her friends would just make it worse, she thought.

"I just got scared from a nightmare."

"Those are the worst! I hate having nightmares. But, luckily for you, I'll never leave you!" Pinkie nuzzled Twilight.

Twilight nuzzled her back. "Thank you." More tears escaped her eyes, landing on Pinkie's back.

"I love you, Twilight."

"I love you too, Pinkie."

"I know none of our friends would leave you either!"

Twilight whispered, "What about when you die?"

"What'd you say?" Pinkie asked innocently.

Twilight cried, "I don't deserve friends like you…"

Pinkie put her hoof on Twilight's back. "Of course you do! We all love you so much."

"But…"

Pinkie shushed her. "We love you, Twilight. And you love us. And that's all that matters."

Twilight offered, "Pinkie… if you won't ever leave me… I won't ever leave you."

Pinkie nuzzled Twilight again. "Deal."

***

Twilight trotted down the stairs of her throne and met Pinkie at the bottom. She had shone up and requested a private meeting with Twilight.

"Hello, Pinkie, what brings you here?"

Pinkie seemed nervous as she looked up at Twilight. "Well, I kinda wanted to ask you something super duper important."

"Oh, well I'm all ears for you."

"I would hope that you're all you for me, but ears are fine."

Twilight giggled. "Pinkie, I am all me for you, especially for you."

Pinkie blushed. "Well, I've been doing a lot of thinking since that last sleepover we had… You know the one where we held each other and you were crying…"

"I remember. What about it?"

"I've been feeling different since then, and its about you."

"What kind of different?" Twilight asked, knowing full well the kind of different she meant. Especially since felt the same for the mare.

"I, uh, wanted to ask you—"

Twilight's world exploded. Her heart felt like it was being stabbed by a thousand tiny daggers. The pain radiated to her whole body as she fell to the ground, unable to do anything but writhe in agony.

Pinkie jumped to her side. "Twilight? What's happening!? Twilight? Somepony, help! Twilight needs help!"

Nopony hears her, unfortunately.

Twilight comes back to life. "Rainbow… Dash…"

Pinkie kneels down and holds Twilight. "Rainbow Dash what?"

"Rainbow… Dash… died…"
