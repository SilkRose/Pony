# Deternal

Twilight Sparkle stood in front of her bed as her lifelong friends slept around her. Her horn lit up as her magic reached out, engulfing each of her friends heart in her magic. Lastly, she wrapped her own heart in the same aura.

For a moment, she just stood there, feeling the rhythmic beating of each heart. As time escaped her, she felt as the influence of her magic tuned all six hearts into beating synchronously. She took a deep breath and began to speak the incantations of the spell.

"Lifespans limited by time, link my friends onto mine. A curse of weakness singed on my heart, for upon each death I will hurt."

She felt a weakness in her legs as the realization of what she'd just done hits her. She falls to the floor as tears fall from her eyes. She was never going to outlive her friends, she just had to convince herself it was the right decision.

As she lays there, crying in a pile of herself, her friends take notice. First it was Rainbow Dash, who sat beside her and put a hoof over her back. Second, it was rarity, who tried her best to hug Twilight. Third it was Applejack, who lifted Twilight head. Fourth it was Fluttershy, who wrapped a wing over Twilight back. Fifth, and last, it was Pinkie, who hugged Twilight tight and with love.
