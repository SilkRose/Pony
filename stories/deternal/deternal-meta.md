# Deternal

## Synopsis:
Twilight casts a spell linking her heart to her friends so when the last one dies, she does too.

## Description:
Twilight hoped she wouldn't regret casting the spell, but at least she wouldn't die alone. At least she wouldn't live without her friends.

## Short Description:
Twilight knew she might regret her decision later, but at least she wouldn't die alone.

## Ideas:
- Twilight casts a spell linking her lifespan to her friends.
- When each friend dies Twilight gets weaker.
- Twilight regrets her choice but is unable to remove the spell.
- Twilight gets desperate and decides to put all magic inside of crystals.
- It doesn't work and Twilight dies with her last friend.
- Pinkie is her last friend alive, who she has been dating since after she cast the spell?
