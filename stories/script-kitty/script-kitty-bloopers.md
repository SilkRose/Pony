# Script Kitty Bloopers

## Buffer Overflow

… She closed the terminal and logged out of Rarity's computer before going back downstairs to check on her friends.

Both of them were smiling.

Rarity said, "Wow, it's making a cup! Did you do something to fix it?"

Opal meowed knowingly.

Fluttershy shook her head. "I don't know, but it's working now."

Opal meowed at Fluttershy after getting back on her seat.

Fluttershy started to pet Opal.

"Let's have coffee, then."

A few seconds later the coffee overflowed the cup and started spilling into the drip guard on the machine.

Rarity asked in a high pitch, "How do you make it stop?"

Fluttershy grabbed another cup and switched them out. "I don't know."

*Guess it doesn't automatically turn off.*

Opal stood up and walked into the other room.

*Eh, I tried. Not my problem now.*
