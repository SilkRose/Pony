# Slow Motion Surprise Party

Fluttershy stood over her garden, she had just finished weeding and was admiring the progress on her plants. Feeling something attaching itself around her back leg, she turned to see a sloth was now clinging to her.

Fluttershy asked, "Hello there critter, what's your name?"

the sloth answered, practically in slow motion, "I'm Lola."

"It's a pleasure to meet you Lola. Can I help you with something?"

"I would like some food."

"Oh, I can definitely help with that. Do you want to come with me in my cottage and I can get you a big plate of leafy greens?"

"That sounds great."

Lola let go of her leg and started to follow her. Unfortunately she was a lot slower than Fluttershy.

"Do you want me to carry you in?"

She nodded. "Yes."

Fluttershy picked up Lola and carried her gently inside. She sat Lola down at her dinning table and began preparing a plate of food for her.

Lola said, "Thank you for your kindness."

Fluttershy had prepared the plate and set it in front of her before she even finished her sentence.

"You're welcome. What brought you to Ponyville?"

"I heard about a big party the town throws for pets and animals."

Thinking for a second, Fluttershy realized what she was referring to. "You mean the annual Ponyville Pet Party. That was over two months ago, you missed it."

Lola frowned. "I miss every party."

"Oh, that's a shame."

"I even miss my own parties."

Fluttershy gasped. "The ones you plane yourself?"

Lola nodded as she continued to eat.

"Oh, my."

*I knew sloths didn't move very fast but two months late is crazy.*

"Where were you coming from?"

"Western Equestria."

*Maybe we can throw her a party that she can't miss.*

"Do you want to stay with me for a bit? I take care of all kinds of animals."

Lola smiled. "Sure."

Fluttershy hugged Lola. "Yay."

After the hug was over, Fluttershy excused herself, "I need to go for now. You can make yourself at home while I'm gone."

Lola nodded. "Bye."

***

Fluttershy entered Pinkie's bedroom. "Pinkie?"

Pinkie bounced from where she was sitting over to Fluttershy. "Fluttershy!"

"I need to ask for your help."

"Oh, what for? I love helping my friends."

"A sloth showed up at my house. She was trying to go to the Ponyville Pet Party that happened a few months ago."

"Oh, so you want to throw a party for your new sloth friend since she missed the town's?"

Fluttershy smiled. "Yes, exactly!"

"I'd love to help! This sounds super fun, but what do sloths like at their party?"

Fluttershy has an idea. "Why don't you come with me and we can try to ask her what she likes without ruining the surprise?"

Pinkie nodded. "Lead the way!"

Fluttershy lead them towards her house and they talked about their plan and slots on the way.

"Now, you might think it would be easy to surprise a sloth with a party, but I don't think it will be that easy."

Pinkie was confused. "Oh, why?"

"While they do move very slow, they are just as smart as any other critter."

Pinkie nodded.

"Wherever they are at the time of the party is where the party will have to happen."

Pinkie nodded again.

"But, how do you set up a whole party where she is when we have to do it all without her noticing?"

"I'm sure we'll think of something!"

Fluttershy smiled. "Thank you for helping."

"You're welcome!"

"I don't even know what kind of games a sloth could do. Wouldn't they move too slow for a piñata?"

"I can make a special one that breaks from the slightest touch. They're used for little little ponies that can't whack the stick very hard."

"Yay!"

"Pin the tail on the pony should be fine."

"That's true."

"I think this is going to be a really fun and memorable party!"

"Me too."

***

Fluttershy introduced her two friends. "Pinkie, this is Lola the sloth. Lola this is my friend Pinkie."

Pinkie said, "Nice to meet you, Lola."

Lola said, "Nice to meet you too."

Fluttershy translated for Pinkie going forward.

Lola made her way to Fluttershy and clung to her leg. Fluttershy blushed slightly. "Sloths like to cling to ponies, especially their caretaker or ponies they feel safe around."

Pinkie asked, "I've never met a sloth before. Can I ask you some questions?"

Lola nodded.

"What's your favorite color?"

"Green."

"Mine is pink!"

Pinkie giggled and both Fluttershy and Lola joined in.

Pinkie asked her next question, "I work at Sugarcube Corner in town, its a bakery and sweets shop. So, I like to ask about food. What's your favorite sweet?"

"I haven't had many sweets to tell you the truth. I would like to try some cake and cupcakes. I don't know if Fluttershy mentioned, but I've actually never been to a party."

Pinkie tried to hide her cutie mark. "Oh, wow. I love cake and cupcakes, I bet you'd love them too!"

Lola nodded.

"How long you going to be staying with Fluttershy? Maybe next time I come over I can bring you a cupcake."

Lola shrugged. "I'm not sure, probably a until I hear about another party."

Pinkie nodded. "I gotta go for now, but it was nice meeting you Lola, and it was nice hanging out Fluttershy."

Lola said, "Goodbye."

Fluttershy said, "You too! See you later."

***

Over the next few days they prepped for the party.

Fluttershy watched over Lola and kept track of the places she frequented. She also talked with her other animals to get them on board with the party, and asked them for help.

Pinkie prepped the party decorations and games. She made the easy-bursting piñata. She got her party canon ready, and baked Lola a cake and many flavors of cupcakes to try.

***

After all the preparation Fluttershy made her way to Sugarcube Corner once again. She trotted through the doors and went up the stares.

Fluttershy entered Pinkie's room again. "Pinkie?"

Pinkie bounced over to her and gave her a hug. "Fluttershy!"

Fluttershy returned the hug. "Is everything for the party ready?"

Pinkie nodded. "I've got everything ready on my end. How about your animals? And Lola?"

"They're all on board, and Lola doesn't suspect a thing."

"Good. I've been thinking about how we can pull off the surprise!"

Fluttershy was relieved. "That's good. I've been trying to think of something."
