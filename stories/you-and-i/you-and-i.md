# You and I

You teleport yourself from Carousel Boutique to the landing just in front of Pinkie's bedroom door. The teleportation leaves you dizzy so you lean against the door until you get your bearings.

I look up from my pillow at the sound of somepony teleporting. I wonder who could that be, maybe Twilight?

You open the door to find Pinkie laying in bed, she is looking up at you. Her face looks shocked after she realizes it's you. "I'm terribly sorry Darling, I had a big order to fulfill and I needed to get them done tonight."

I smile at you and lift up the blanket, signalling for you to get in the bed. "You're alright, Rarity. I love you and would wait all night for you."

You trot over to the bed and get in, scooting close to Pinkie. "I love you too!"

I wrap my hooves around you, pulling you close. "So, when were you going to tell me you could teleport?"

You blush and nuzzles Pinkie. "Well, I learned it for you… I wanted to be able to get here faster and be able to spend more time with you. It was actually really hard to learn since magic isn't my special talent like Twilight."

It's my turn to blush now. "Wow, you learned to teleport just for me! That's so amazing. I love you!"

You hold Pinkie hoof to your chest. "I love you too, darling!"
