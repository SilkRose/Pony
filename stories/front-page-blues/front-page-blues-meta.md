# Front Page Blues

## Synopsis:
Rarity (as a filly) having her new dress designs make it to a fashion magazine for the first time and is ecstatic, but reaches disappointment when she fails to consistently make it in there.

## Description:
Rarity's dresses never cease to amaze ponies, each one of them stunning and beautiful in their own right. She almost always makes the front page of at least one magazine with them. So, after her latest dress gets featured in the number one magazine in Equestria, why is she crying in her bed?

## Short Description:
Rarity's dresses almost always make it onto the front page of a magazine. So, why does she feel so sad?

## Ideas:
- Rarity's latest dress is doing well, but not as well as she expected.
- She put more time into it as it was special to her.
- It even got featured in the number one magazine.
- She listens too much to the baseless critiques and becomes distraught.
- She cries alone in her bed, lamenting about the time and effort she put in.
- She beats herself up for feeling so bad when the dress has received more positive feedback than negative.
- Pinkie knocks on her door, asking if she is alright.
- Pinkie comes in and they start to talk about creativity and expectations.
- Pinkie explains how she deals with this too when it comes to parties.
- Rarity calms down and they come to an understanding.
- They hug.
