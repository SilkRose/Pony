# Heavy Weighs a Frown

Heavy weighs the frown, that held Pinkie Pie down. She did not know what made her so low.

When ponies ask if she's fine, she wouldn't reveal what's really on the mind.

She hid, and fibbed, and did. Everything to slink away from her own kind.

Ponies did pry, others wouldn't try. When asked, this Pie would just lie.
