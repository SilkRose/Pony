# Heavy Weighs a Frown

Heavy weighs the frown that held Pinkie Pie down. For all of the day, things did not go her way. She woke up feeling great, only to have to wait. Customers came and customers went, but all she wanted to do was vent.

Once her shift was over, she knew just where to go. If anything could help her, Twilight would know. She ran to her friend's, now that work had come to an end. She tripped on a rug, but landed on Twilight with a hug.

They talked for a while, for what felt like a mile, but nothing could make Pinkie smile. Her friend said she knew the way, for Pinkie just had to play. Go see Rainbow Dash, you'll have fun in a flash.

She left with a hug, watching out for the rug for she had a friend to go bug. Looking all over town while she wore a frown. with no luck she went to her friend's that could buck. She found Rainbow in a tree, but could not let her be.

…

She hid, and fibbed, and did. Everything to slink away from her own kind. Ponies did pry, others wouldn't try. When asked, this Pie would just lie.
