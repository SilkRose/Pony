# Celestia → Discord ([RunicTreetops](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/489485/RunicTreetops))

Discord,

I hope this letter finds you well. Actually, I hope it finds you at all. You are not an easy draconequus to track down, let alone find a mailing address for. I understand that last time I attempted to do so, the poor stallion that delivered your letter was not only late, but wound up missing for several months after the fact. I can only imagine that we have your “interesting” choice of living arrangements to thank for that. Measures have been taken to ensure that this letter arrives with fewer complications, and for good measure, I included next year’s gala tickets as well to avoid having to send another later on. Please do not lose them!

I’d ask how you’ve been, but we both know Twilight has been keeping me up to date in that regard. What was the term she used again? “Using the thin line between harmless fun and genuine danger as a jump rope on a daily basis?” Yes, that sounds correct. So, how has that been treating you? Have you gotten accustomed to the chaos that is an average life in Equestria?

If I may be so bold as to open up a little to the “Lord of Chaos,” I will admit that I was worried you might be unhappy with this life. It’s certainly not what an outsider would expect from someone of your stature or background, at least. For a time I thought you might become dissatisfied or, arguably worse, bored. It took little more than a glance at the many letters Twilight has sent me about the adventures she and her friends get up to to remind me that those fears were unfounded.

Besides, it seems to me that Fluttershy has proven to be more than enough to keep you happy. Feel free to argue that point, though. I’ve been wrong before.

In the name of friendship, I would like to say one more thing: Whether or not your pride will allow you to admit it, you’ve brought both great evil and great joy to my little ponies. Lately, I’ve heard almost exclusively the latter, though you *do* occasionally fall somewhere in the middle. I do not wish to condemn you for past actions; I’m sure you’ve done that yourself plenty of times already. All I ask is that you keep doing what you’re doing. I think your influence can be quite healthy for Equestria, at least in small doses. You’re capable of even more than you think, Discord. Use that power well.

If nothing else, please consider taking my invitation to next year’s gala seriously. It’s much more fun with you around!

Now, to fulfil my duty as another link in this chain and pass on the ever-growing list of advice!

Pinkie: "Friendship is like a party, both are fun and lead to smiles."

Applejack: "It's always worth it to give your family and friends a little love whenever you see them."

Fluttershy: "Hug your closest pony."

Rainbow Dash: "Don't be afraid to ask for help; even somepony as cool as me, or as cool as you, cannot be expected to solve every problem on their own."

Spike: "Words are strong."

Starlight Glimmer: “There's a world outside your head. Don't be afraid to let it in.”

If I may save you the trouble of summarizing my own words, allow me to do it for you: “Be not defined by your past mistakes. After all, the sun will always rise again. Today is a new day!”

With care,  
Princess Celestia
