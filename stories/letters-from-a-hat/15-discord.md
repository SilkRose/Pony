# Discord → Twilight Sparkle ([SparklingTwilight](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/423060/SparklingTwilight))

Twi||ligh||t Sparkle,

For this "Friendship" project, I suppose I must review a checklist for a really boring section of pass-through messages. Let us then get this inanity out of the way early, so we can bore everycreature before there is a risk that those creatures might peruse the amazing wisdom hidden within my erudite writings.

Pinkie prattled: "Friendship is like a potty, both are fun and lead to smiles." Check.

Applejack added: ".meht ees uoy revenehw evol elttil a sdneirf dna ylimaf ruoy evig ot ti htrow syawla s'tI", check.

Fluttershy wishes to relate that we all should: "Hug your closest pony." Check! Excellent advice. I just returned from hugging her, myself. Fluttershy is **always** my nearest pony. Because she lives in my heart.

Rainbow Dash deigned: "Don't be afraid to ask for help. Rainbow Dash cannot be expected to solve every problem on her own."

Spike shouted with extreme confidence: "**WORDS ARE STRONG**."

Starlight Glimmer susurrated: “There's a world outside your head. Need to be careful around that one. Just last month I helped her out of a sticky situation involving an ungentlemanly chap with tentacles protruding from everywhere who was threatening to--well, that's neither here nor there and it's not my story to tell of how she got herself entangled in that situation, is it? By chaos, was she ever twisted in a pickle. And I am _certain_ you have heard all about it already.

I thought the whole purpose of saving her from Nightmare Moon was because Equestria would otherwise be shrouded in eternal darkness? Hem-hem.

**Discord: In Sincerely**

All that said, I, of course, realize it is indubitably impossible to ignore that any third party spying on our correspondence may be a tad bit confused at the perfidy inherent in poorly-conceptualized poetry constructed in a post-modernist fashion. Spoof and parody are a rarity, and I am not talking about the self-same pony's deceased womb-friend twin [who was eaten](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/23023-vanishing-twin-syndrome). Neigh, I speak about many things. Cabbages and queens. Besides, poor grammar and ill-considered parody are something that some**one** would expect of me. But I am more than a million monkeys typing at a million typewriters--a typewriter being a magically propelled **type** of quill that writes. I am more, far more than artificial unintelligence. I am unpredictable. And so, I will speak--neigh--write in a way that you would not expect.

Indeed, Twit Sparkle--sorry for the ink-drop obliterating part of your name--I am auto-dictating. What I state, my typewriter writes--the proper types of letters. 真的吗 小马, 我不是你的妈妈. I hope you can understand that, dear T, 呢.

What I mean to say, that is, what I prepared to say, is T.S.! I want to be your friend, whether you want it or not, T.S. But, I don't know how.

P.S. Most ponies are only worth half a penny-farthing. Two bits is a lot.

I wish I could have written to Fluttershy. But I am willing to humble myself by writing you. Because you are who was assigned. It is _quite_ a pity that the joy of writing to Fluttershy was already "taken"--the word makes me shudder--by Applejack. Who knows what perversions of pitiable prose that peasant pony piled upon Fluttershy's pure palatial and proverbial palate? One shudders thinking of the udder lack of grammatical knowledge that a cow-milker would exude. Applejack could never satisfy Fluttershy's need for prescient popular perspicacity!

Still, at least **you** were available and I could rig the wheel of fortune--claw into the bag of names and pick you out despite the Pink One's probability-affecting cloud of chaos. Moment of advice, as I am also a pretty-pertinent prognosticator--you should watch that one lest she become [a chaos goddess herself](https://www.fimfiction.net/story/482629/the-41-second-goddess) in some future season.

But WHY did I rig the metaphorical wheel? How could I, the very model of a modern major moralist, execute such an "unethical" action? Because I have something to say to you, Dear Twi.

And isn't that really what friendship is?

Crime?

Be happy and do crime--isn't that the saying? Or, perhaps I was cogitating--neigh--contemplating a synonym? Drat. All that thinking about Applejack caused me to lose some brain cells... But one supposes one must provide a fair analysis. Applejack is... hard working. And Fluttershy likes her. So, yes, I do like her. I do not love everything she does--far from it! But I respect her. Sometimes, I flatter Flutters so much that I run out of positive things to say, and so I tear other ponies down. It is not chaotic to keep doing the same thing all the time--though it can be for a while. I do not wish to fade away. *Sigh*. To live in a soSIGHety...

P.P.P.P.P.P.P.P.S.S.S.S.S.S.S.S.S.S.: Maybe someday we can get apple cider from Applejack. Pay her for it, you know. Fairly and all, to make up for my rudeness. Applejack would love that--assuming she is not sold out. She really should look into hiring more employees during that time of year. And if we buy that excess cider she produces, doing so would be being a pal, a friend, a chum. And then we prank Celestia. She loves pranks. Always has. I remember the time she stuck a "kick me" sticker on her sister's bumper and then I kissed it--because why would I do precisely what somecreature says--and she bucked and they trapped me in a statue for a thousand years... Yes, pranking her back would be an excellent friendship-building experience.

I also wrote to Fluttershy. Just for fun. Because I was annoyed. Why could I not reach into the sack and lie about which name I drew? Not impossible... **EXCEPT FLUTTERSHY ALREADY WENT!** NO ONE TOLD ME FLUTTERSHY HAD ALREADY WRITTEN WHEN I AGREED TO PARTICIPATE IN THIS FRIENDSHIP-BUILDING EXERCISE!

P.P.S. Can you send a message to Cadance faster than I can? I just let her know we made up--Fluttershy will love that! And that we were pregnant with my foals. Twins. And were going in to labor. Because draconeqqui are sigma strong like that. The whole foal-bearing experience happens in a blink of an eye. Our foals will be named Fluttershy2 and Sp!kez00s and TurokXAEAppleHitter and a symbol that looks like Celestia's bum. Because our Twins will be conjoined. And we need foalsitting bad because I'm getting hungry and might eat them. Because our pregnancy was really difficult. And there's a prophecy that one of my children might overthrow me and destroy the world with crazy lightning powers. (That's probably Sp!kez00s. He's a wily one.)

Writing to somecreature who does not trust you, who doesn't like you, but who sometimes could greatly benefit from putting a smile on her oft-distressed face because she's tired from saving the world with little help from anypony else except the gallant, generous, genial, gracious Fluttershy.

Thank you for watching out for her: Fluttershy.

We may not be close.

But Fluttershy values you.

So I do too.

You're worth two bits.
