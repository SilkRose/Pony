# Celestia → Starlight ([RunicTreetops](https://www.fimfiction.net/user/489485/RunicTreetops))

My (newly) faithful subject Starlight Glimmer,

Let me begin by first addressing your concerns, as I imagine you have probably been sweating bullets since Spike handed you this very letter. Allow me to be blunt for the sake of your nerves: No, I am not upset with you in any way, nor are you in any trouble. The lovely thing about Equestria’s postal system is that letters are a form of intimate conversation, one which cannot be overheard by curious ears. Princess or otherwise, our conversations are our own, and to that end, I appreciate your honesty and willingness to be emotionally open with somepony like me. Very few are, nowadays.

It fills me with joy to see just how much Twilight has affected you. I understand that it’s far from an uncommon belief to think that I know everything, and while I must admit a certain level of, shall we say, “experience” treats me well in my day-to-day affairs, not even I can read minds. The world makes sense. Science makes sense. With enough study, even magic makes sense. But ponies? Ponies are surprising. Can I predict or, dare I say, *hope* that my faith in my subjects will not go unrewarded? Absolutely, but I cannot *know*. To that end, while I have never doubted my faithful student for even a moment, she has somehow managed to surprise and impress me time and time again.

And so have you, Starlight.

It’s no small feat to do what you have. You needn’t look any further than our “bug queen” to see that you have succeeded where others have not. I had hoped beyond hope when I read Twilight’s reports that you would grow as she believed you could. I am nothing short of elated to see that you’ve far exceeded expectations. Perhaps the two of you are more alike than you realize, hm? Keep your chin up, Starlight. There are still many great things you’ve yet to see, yet to learn. Surprises are a good thing, from buckball to… well, friendship! Take them in stride, and keep moving forward.

One final thing, if I may. I understand where you’re coming from more than you could possibly know. The day I lost my sister was, undoubtedly, the worst day of my life. Had she made mistakes? Yes. Had I made just as many, enough to push her towards her eventual fate? Yes. From the moment she was gone, I felt nothing but sorrow in my heart. For one-thousand years, I wondered what could have been, what I might have done differently. More than anything, I wondered what my dearest sister might think of me. However, what was done was done. I could do nothing but move forward, using everything at my disposal to create an Equestria where such a mistake would not - could not - be repeated. It’s a feeling that, even now, hasn’t quite gone away. 

I am no stranger to guilt, Starlight. Please believe me when I say that it is both an enemy you shall never truly defeat, and an ally you can always look towards for guidance. You have done great things, and I have no doubt that you will do many more. My door is always open to you if ever you need it. 

And between you and me, I’ve got a personal recipe for hot cocoa that is simply to die for.

With care,  
Princess Celestia
