# Chaotic Crush

## Synopsis:
Discord has a crush on Fluttershy and asks all of her friends for advice before just realizing that Twilight's advice was the best.

## Description:
Discord asks Fluttershy's friends for advice on his feelings for Fluttershy. Which advice will he follow?

- Twilight tells discord he just needs to talk to Fluttershy about his feelings and Discord blows her off saying there's no way it could be that simple.
- Twilight: read this book about romance (he follow's it verbatim)
- Rarity: take her on a date
- Pinkie: give her a gift
- Rainbow: woo her with a show
