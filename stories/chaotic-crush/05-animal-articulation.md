# Animal Articulation

Fluttershy follows a trio of birds as they lead her back to her home. They had told her they needed her for something but didn't say what it was.

As they approached the house, she could hear a bunch of animal noises coming from inside. After trotting inside, all her animals friends starting cheering for her. Sitting on her couch was Discord.

Fluttershy asks, "What's going on in here?"

Discord snapped his fingers and nothing seems to happen. "I needed to talk to you, and so did your animal friends, or well, I should say our animal friends, but I'll let them explain that part."

A few seconds later, one of the birds that brought Fluttershy back home started speaking. "Discord has made an effort to befriend us. While we didn't believe he was serious at first, he's done a good job and seems to care for all of us."

Fluttershy says, "Oh wow! You made it so my animals can speak? Did you make them say that?"

Angel speaks up, "He didn't make them say anything, I still hate him."
