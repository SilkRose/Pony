# Catch me Fainting

She caught me like it was just another thing she'd do. Like we were in love but didn't know it yet. I'm set down gently onto the original target I had for my dramatic faint as she looks at me with a warm smile and wonder in her eyes.

I would have said something dramatic, but now, I'm left speechless as I stare at Pinkie Pie. She casually trots away doing what she was doing before she held me gently. As a tinge of a blush comes over me, I don't know what to think.

I had already forgot whatever minor inconvenience had me dramatically fainting onto my couch in the first place. For this was far more important. This was far more fun.
